## Deliverable (what "done" looks like)
成功標準：觀看自己的 Loom 影片，確認簡報開始的 **60 秒內**已經秀出最具震撼力的商業結果或解決方案終點。5-min Loom total, value hit inside first 60 seconds.

## Capacity slot

## Sub-steps

- [ ] Record a 5-minute micro demo.
- [ ] First 60 seconds: show the final value hit directly.
- [ ] Remaining 4 minutes: deconstruct which back-end clicks produced that value.
- [ ] Review the recording and confirm the value hit lands inside 60 seconds.
