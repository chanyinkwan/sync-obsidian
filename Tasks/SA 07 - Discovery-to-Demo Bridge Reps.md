# SA 07 - Discovery-to-Demo Bridge Reps

母任務:[[SA Presales Transition]]
Source: [[how to become a Solution Architect or Presales Consultant]] — 行動6：反覆演練「從需求挖掘到產品展示」的橋樑語句 (Discovery-to-Demo Bridge)

Transition formula: 「既然您提到了 [A 痛點] 導致了 [B 商業損失]，那我們今天的展示會直奔主題，專注在 [C 功能] 如何幫您拿回 [D 商業利益]。」

## Deliverable (what "done" looks like)
成功標準：聽自己的錄音，過渡句在 **30 秒內**完成，語調自信、毫無結巴或 "uh/and/so" 等冗詞贅字。Not done until all 5 recorded reps meet this bar.

## Capacity slot

## Sub-steps

- [ ] Record each rep with a phone voice recorder.

## Rep counter
Reps = the 5 daily recorded instances (Mon–Fri of W4); completion is tracked per instance in TaskNotes. Each instance is one scenario recorded — ≤30s transition, no filler words.
