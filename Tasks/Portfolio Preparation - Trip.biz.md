### S1 — Huawei: turning an APAC HQ roadmap into 5-market EMEA reality
*Portfolio Solutions & Commercial Sales Specialist · London · May 2026 – present*

- **Pain:** A China HQ product roadmap set for global volume, versus a Tier-1 European mobile operator group buying across 5 country markets with its own commercial logic.
- **Goal:** Win the pursuit without asking HQ to build a Europe-only product line.
- **Mechanism:** Ran the full requirements cycle across 3 technical workshops plus a structured analysis session, turning customer input into documented solution scope; validated feasibility across 6 account subnets before proposal commitment; then argued a low-value feature out of the device configuration through HQ's GTM roadmap and pricing reviews on the evidence it carried no weight in the customer's buying decision.
- **Outcome:** USD 2 per unit of product cost removed; owns bid responses and pricing analysis for the group, coordinating a squad of account managers, channel GTM ops and technical architects.
- **Use for:** Q1 (HQ vs local roadmap conflict), Q2 (convincing Shanghai/Singapore engineering), Q9 (RFP structure).
- **Line that lands:** "I didn't ask HQ for a European product. I asked them to drop something Europe was paying for and nobody was buying."

### S2 — Bank of China: BOC Pay shipped across 4 regions on a regulatory clock
*Technical Product Manager, E-Banking · Hong Kong · Aug 2023 – Oct 2024*

- **Pain:** An HKMA account-protection mandate with a hard 14-day deadline, on a product launching simultaneously across 4 global regions with teams in different timezones.
- **Goal:** Full compliance and a clean multi-region launch without burning the manday budget.
- **Mechanism:** Translated the regulation into a technical implementation plan, aligned internal teams and third-party vendors through technical workshops, and shipped Geo-IP fraud detection with Impossible Travel logic; designed a follow-the-sun 24-hour synchronised UAT schedule off a timezone gap analysis so no region sat idle.
- **Outcome:** 100% of HKMA mandates met inside 14 days; launch across 4+ regions with no handover gaps; 15 mandays returned to the project budget.
- **Use for:** Q7 (Betriebsrat privacy design), Q8 (PSD3/SCA), Q1 (cross-region coordination).
- **Line that lands:** "Compliance wasn't the constraint on the roadmap — it was the feature that made the deal legal."
- **Evidence flag:** the 15-manday and follow-the-sun figures are marked `needs_grounding` in MasterExperienceDB. Say "roughly two weeks of budget" unless you can re-verify.

### S3 — Bank of China: productising 50+ manual reporting rituals
*Technical Product Manager, E-Banking · Hong Kong · Aug 2023 – Oct 2024*

- **Pain:** System health depended on inconsistent manual "ping" protocols and hand-assembled weekly reports across 20 systems and 50+ teams — slow detection, no single source of truth.
- **Goal:** Cut cost-to-serve on monitoring without adding headcount.
- **Mechanism:** Built a centralised Grafana + Prometheus observability framework that replaced fragmented manual checks with real-time signals in one dashboard, then drove adoption by demoing it live to 30+ system leads, the UX department head and the networking lead.
- **Outcome:** 50+ manual weekly reporting touchpoints retired; ~8 hours a week of manual reporting effort recovered (~400 hours a year); most technical objections resolved in the first session.
- **Use for:** Q3 (productising Key Travel's manual account book), Q4 (AI-to-human handoff design).
- **Line that lands:** "The migration risk wasn't technical. It was that 50 teams each owned a spreadsheet, so I made the dashboard easier than the spreadsheet."
- **Evidence flag:** the "80% of queries resolved" and "20 hours weekly across 50+ teams" numbers vary between sources. Use the ~400 hours/year figure — it's the verified one.

### S4 — Apple: reversing the Shanghai HQ decision, then landing the POS migration
*Operations Data Analyst · Hong Kong · Mar 2021 – Aug 2023*

- **Pain:** Shanghai HQ mandated centralised, one-size-fits-all training for the EasyPay POS migration; store operations actually varied by location, so the rollout was set to break on the floor.
- **Goal:** Migrate the payment system with zero downtime and no customer-satisfaction hit.
- **Mechanism:** Presented evidence of store-level operational variance and moved the Shanghai development team onto store-specific delivery; converted technical requirements into floor-level playbooks and prepared 10+ Floor Champions; when store leaders demanded a rollback mid-migration, showed them the original report flow still ran as a fallback and committed technical support through the transition.
- **Outcome:** NPS held at 92, full operational uptime through the migration, adoption secured across all 6 flagship stores.
- **Use for:** Q1/Q2 (influencing HQ without authority), Q4 (service redesign without breaking trust), Q3 (change management on a migration).
- **Line that lands:** "HQ wasn't wrong about the product. They were wrong about the assumption that every store runs the same way — so I brought the data, not the complaint."
- **Evidence flag:** NPS 92 is marked `needs_grounding`. Safer phrasing: "customer satisfaction held through the migration."

### S5 — Apple: £350K B2B pipeline built account by account
*B2B Solution Expert · Hong Kong · Jan 2020 – Mar 2021*

- **Pain:** No systematic corporate pipeline in the region; the team tracked revenue reactively and lost accounts before anyone noticed.
- **Goal:** Build a repeatable commercial motion, not one-off wins.
- **Mechanism:** Ran discovery, solution positioning and qualification end to end across a 50+ account portfolio, escalating matured accounts to corporate sales; built diagnostic models that isolated 12 root causes of revenue variance; on the Viu TV deal, worked a walk-in store visit into a qualified opportunity over 3 months, built credibility through hands-on technical support and a custom business pricing portal, then cleared the device-management objection by upselling MDM.
- **Outcome:** £350,000 in new B2B revenue, 30% lift in regional market penetration, £50K Viu TV close.
- **Use for:** Q9 (presales to procurement), Q5 (unit economics and cost-to-serve), Q10 (differentiating on buyer criteria).
- **Line that lands:** "The pricing portal wasn't a sales tool. It was the product artifact that made procurement say yes."
