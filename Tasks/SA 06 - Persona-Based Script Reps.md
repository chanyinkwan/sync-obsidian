# SA 06 - Persona-Based Script Reps

母任務:[[SA Presales Transition]]
Source: [[how to become a Solution Architect or Presales Consultant]] — 行動5：精通「基於特定角色的提問腳本」(Persona-Based Script)

## Deliverable (what "done" looks like)
成功標準：面對不同的虛擬角色時，能夠在 **3 秒鐘內**脫口而出 2 個專屬於該角色痛點的精準提問（3-second test）。Not done until the rep count below is hit.

## Capacity slot
Overlay W3.

## Sub-steps
- [ ] Re-sort the 21 questions from SA 05 into three tags: 【CFO/CIO 懂的語言】、【架構師在乎的細節】、【日常使用者（PM/工程師）的煩惱】.
- [ ] Practise switching tone of voice while looking at each persona's avatar, translating technical terms into business value (e.g. "降低延遲" → "提高結帳轉化率").

## Rep counter
Reps = the 5 daily instances (Mon–Fri of W3); completion is tracked per instance in TaskNotes. Each instance is one switching drill across all 3 personas; the final instance must pass the 3-second test.
