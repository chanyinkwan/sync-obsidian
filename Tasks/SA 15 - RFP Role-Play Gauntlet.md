# SA 15 - RFP Role-Play Gauntlet

母任務:[[SA Presales Transition]]
Source: [[how to become a Solution Architect or Presales Consultant]] — 行動12：全面轉向「情境模擬」模擬面試 (Role-Play Interviewing)

Use the mirrofish × AI voice-agent idea from the source note's Seed idea section to run role-play scenarios across the sales lifecycle.

## Deliverable (what "done" looks like)
成功標準：完成至少 **3 場**高壓模擬對抗，且回看錄影時，你面對客戶的挑剔沒有做出任何「技術讓步」或「情緒防衛」，而是每次都用商業影響力（P&L）把話題引導回來。Recordings show zero technical concessions and zero emotional defensiveness — every challenge redirected to business impact / P&L.

## Capacity slot
Spans W9–W10 (base layer, role-play sessions alongside applications).

## Sub-steps
- [ ] Pull a real enterprise RFP template (or download a standard technical requirements checklist online).
- [ ] Line up a technically sharp friend or an advanced AI (mirrofish × voice agent) to play "an extremely picky, budget-constrained customer CTO".
- [ ] Run a 20-minute online session defending the RFP, holding the sales stance (maintain control, no over-promising, redirect to business value).

## Rep counter
Recurrence yields 4 slots (Tue/Thu of W9–W10: 09-08, 09-10, 09-15, 09-17); completion is tracked per instance in TaskNotes. Minimum 3 completed reps required — the 4th slot is buffer. Slots deliberately avoid the 09-11 → 09-13 sprint weekend (SA 14). Each instance: 20-min RFP defense, recorded, reviewed for technical concessions / emotional defensiveness.
