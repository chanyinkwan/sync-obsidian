# SA 11 - 48hr Deconstruction Sprint 1 - Domain Leader

母任務:[[SA Presales Transition]]
Source: [[how to become a Solution Architect or Presales Consultant]] — 行動11：徹底執行 48 小時產品拆解 SOP (48-Hour Product Deconstruction) — first run.

Weekend sprint (Friday night → Sunday night). Product = the leader of the domain chosen in SA 02.

## Deliverable (what "done" looks like)
成功標準（mirror-test）：在 48 小時截止時，你能對著鏡子流暢地講出一篇 5 分鐘的競爭分析簡報——該產品如何幫客戶賺錢、客戶最討厭它哪一點、我們該如何贏過它。

The 5-phase clock:
- 0–4h: money map (商業模式)
- 4–12h: problem & alternatives (痛點與替代方案調研)
- 12–24h: hands-on (玩免費版)
- 24–36h: persona & discovery pack (提問庫)
- 36–48h: synthesis — value map + 15-min demo narrative + whiteboard from memory

## Capacity slot
Weekend sprint (outside working-hours capacity), sits alongside W7.

## Sub-steps
- [ ] Friday night: select the domain leader product (unfamiliar competitor) to deconstruct.
- [ ] 0–4h: map how the product makes money.
- [ ] 4–12h: research problems and alternatives (e.g. Reddit pain points).
- [ ] 12–24h: hands-on with the free tier.
- [ ] 24–36h: write the persona & discovery question pack.
- [ ] 36–48h: synthesize value map + 15-min demo narrative + whiteboard from memory.
- [ ] Sunday night: deliver the 5-min mirror-test competitive brief.
