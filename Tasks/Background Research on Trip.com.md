Method: 4 parallel web/Playwright research agents, then **four adversarial review passes** producing 9, 10, 3 and 2 blocking corrections respectively. All applied; the material ones are marked inline with a ⚠️ so you can see what was wrong and why it mattered.

Confidence tags on every claim:
- **[FILING]** — SEC filing, earnings release, regulator decision, company registry. Verifiable, adversarial-proof.
- **[CO-CLAIM]** — the company saying something about itself: marketing page, PR, executive interview. Often true, never independently verified, and superlatives here are sales copy. Do not repeat as fact.

- **[WEAK]** — search-snippet only, aggregator, or second-hand. Verify before quoting.
- **[INFER]** — my conclusion, not anyone's claim.
- **[FACT]** / **[FACT via aggregator]** — legacy tags from the first pass, not yet fully migrated. Read them as **[FILING]** where the source is an earnings release, regulator decision or registry, and as **[CO-CLAIM]** where the source is a company PR or marketing page. Where a tag and a source disagree, **trust the source, not the tag.**

> ⚠️ **CORRECTED — the first draft of this section was 18 months out of date.** It said "large companies in scope from 1 Jan 2025, listed SMEs from 1 Jan 2026."
