# 2. Decision\-Making and Influence Chain Mapping

**Purpose**

This assessment is designed to systematically map the Device Business decision\-making process across each strategic domain — Devices, AI, Cloud, Consumer Business, Ecosystem and Procurement —, executive landscape, governance forums, procurement influence, approval chains, successor planning, Huawei relationship coverage, and executive engagement opportunities. The output will support executive relationship mapping, decision\-chain mapping, relationship reach\-rate dashboards, succession planning and annual executive engagement programs.

Key dimensions addressed to be fulfilled by Key Account:

**1. Key Account Overview**

Capture Operator Group, OpCos covered, Huawei Account Director, reporting period, executive sponsor, overall account health and strategic priorities.

**2. Executive Landscape Mapping**

Map Group and OpCo executives including CEO, CFO, CTO, CIO, Procurement, Device, Consumer, Innovation and Strategy leaders. Assess influence, Huawei relationship status, sponsor and priority.

**3. Decision\-Making Chain Mapping**

Document complete decision chain from CEO to operational decision owners. Include approval routes for devices, AI, ecosystem and procurement.

**4. Stakeholder Categorization**

Classify all stakeholders as Decision Maker, Influencer, Gatekeeper, Champion or Opponent/Blocker.

**5. Strategic Domain Decision Ownership**

Document ownership and approval process for Smartphones, Devices, AI, Customer Experience, Cloud, Ecosystem and Procurement.

**6. Governance and Approval Forums**

Identify Executive Committees, Commercial Boards, Technology Boards, Procurement Boards, Investment Committees, Innovation Councils and Security Boards.

**7. Device Business Approval Process**

Document smartphone portfolio approval, ranging decisions, subsidy approvals, vendor onboarding and contract signature workflows.

**8. Strategic Decision Chain Mapping by Domain**

Map Devices, AI, Cloud, Ecosystem and Procurement decision chains and identify key approval gates.

**10. Huawei Relationship Coverage**

Assess CEO, Board, CTO, Procurement, Device Business, Innovation and OpCo access. Calculate Relationship Reach Rate.
