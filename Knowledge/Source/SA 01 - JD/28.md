## About the job

Presales Manager (AI Solutions) opportunity with an expanding AI SaaS product house based in London, Battersea (SW11) – hybrid working model (3 days onsite)

  

Our client is part of a multi-national committed to innovation and technology, with operations in over 50 countries. They benefit from data, expertise and IP on a conglomerate scale while operating as an agile startup to create a suite of AI products for large enterprises

  

This role may suit someone who wants to transition from consulting to industry, and salary guide is c£85K + 10% bonus + pension + health care

  

Responsibilities:

- Working with clients, account teams and product solution teams to identify and architect appropriate digital solutions.
- Prepare proposals, presentations and support the negotiation of contracts to successful closure
- Provide market feedback of competing solutions as well as Industry trends
- Deliver customised demonstrations and workshops
- Maintain pricing models for major bids

  

Requirements

- Previous sales / Presales experience of Data / AI Solutions into large enterprises
- Profession services / consulting experience working with enterprise Clients ideally (Utilities, infrastructure projects, smart city, semi state etc.)
- Experience in selling complex and rapidly evolving solutions
- Solid understanding of data analytics technologies including main data science and big data platforms
- Excellent document writing and creation skills
