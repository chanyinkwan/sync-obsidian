## Your future duties and responsibilities:

In this role, you will lead the Global Technology Operations (GTO) solution for major multi-million-pound bids, taking ownership of the end-to-end solution throughout the pre-sales lifecycle. Working closely with Opportunity Pursuit Leads, technical specialists, and business stakeholders, you will ensure solutions are innovative, commercially competitive, and fully aligned to client objectives while meeting governance, quality, and delivery standards.  
You'll coordinate multidisciplinary teams across infrastructure, cloud, networks, end user services, and IT service management, bringing together technical expertise to develop compelling proposals that maximise value for both clients and CGI. You'll also help strengthen our bid capability by sharing knowledge, mentoring colleagues, and driving continuous improvement across the solution development process.  
Key responsibilities:  
• Lead & Own the GTO solution for strategic, high-value bid opportunities from qualification through to contract award.  
• Design & Shape innovative service and technical solutions that align with customer strategies, requirements, and desired outcomes.  
• Coordinate & Deliver multidisciplinary teams, ensuring solution plans, milestones, governance activities, and approvals are completed on time.  
• Manage & Optimise solution costs, pricing inputs, benchmarking activities, and bid budgets to deliver commercially competitive proposals.  
• Review & Assure solution quality through technical reviews, governance processes, and implementation sign-offs.  
• Identify & Mitigate technical, commercial, and delivery risks throughout the bid lifecycle.  
• Present & Influence senior stakeholders through solution reviews, client presentations, and executive briefings.  
• Collaborate & Improve with third-party suppliers, internal delivery teams, and business units to strengthen solution quality and efficiency.  
• Support & Develop colleagues through coaching, knowledge sharing, and participation in lessons learned activities.  
• Enable & Transition successful opportunities by leading solution handovers into delivery teams.

## Required qualifications to be successful in this role:

To be successful in this role, you'll bring significant experience designing complex infrastructure and managed service solutions within large-scale bids. You'll combine broad technical knowledge with commercial awareness, excellent stakeholder management skills, and the ability to lead cross-functional teams in delivering high-quality, customer-focused solutions.  
Essential qualifications and experience:  
• Extensive experience in Solution Architecture, Solution Ownership, or Technical Pre-Sales within complex IT services or managed services environments.  
• Strong knowledge across infrastructure, cloud, hosting, networks, end user computing, and IT service management.  
• Proven experience leading technical solution development for large, complex bid opportunities.  
• Strong understanding of commercial models, cost estimation, pricing principles, and bid financial management.  
• Experience presenting technical solutions to senior client stakeholders and executive leadership teams.  
• Demonstrated ability to manage technical and non-technical risks across complex solution designs.  
• Excellent communication, facilitation, and proposal writing skills.  
• Experience working collaboratively with multidisciplinary teams and third-party suppliers.  
• Strong organisational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities and governance requirements.
