# Amazon Web Services (AWS) — Solutions Architect, AWS Telco Business Unit

## Signal notes

**AWS's own Solutions Architect req names zero AWS certification.** Basic Qualifications ask for "background in Cloud Architecture... or DevOps" and "literacy in GenAI" — not a single cert, not even "AWS Certified," anywhere in Basic or Preferred Qualifications. This is a second data point (after the Siemens Grid Software req) suggesting the SAA cert isn't the hard gate the transition plan assumes; the actual gate here looks like hands-on build depth, not a badge.

**This is the most hands-on-technical req of the batch so far.** GenAI literacy plus a Cloud Architecture/Systems Design/DevOps background is asked directly, and there's no RFP, proposal, or demo language anywhere in the posting. Compared to the Infosys and Siemens Capital reqs, which lean commercial or domain, this one sits closest to a pure build-and-advise engineering role — which is exactly the user's stated gap (limited IaC/coding depth).

**Telecom vertical is a real domain-fit signal, not a stretch.** The user already carries telecom account exposure through the team's Account Management Hub (Telefonica and other major telco accounts). If presales-motion skills transfer across verticals the way JD 01's analysis suggests, telecom is the one vertical in this batch where the user has adjacent context instead of a cold domain wall.

**"Single-threaded owner" is a title-inflation flag.** Being sole owner of an account's entire AWS technology strategy usually reads as principal-SA scope at AWS, but no seniority modifier appears in the title itself — I'm calling seniority unclear rather than assigning senior/principal outright, since the level band that would resolve it isn't present in the posting.

### Description

Amazon Web Services (AWS) is seeking a Solutions Architect to partner with the world's leading Telecommunications companies on their most ambitious cloud transformation journeys.

The problem domains are diverse: Enterprise IT Modernization, Data and GenAI, Cybersecurity, 5G monetization, digital customer journeys, OSS/BSS transformation, cybersecurity. At your disposal is the breadth of AWS.

You will leverage the full breadth of AWS services—including AI/ML, edge computing, IoT, and advanced analytics—to craft solutions that address real telecommunications challenges.

### Key job responsibilities

As part of the team, you will collaborate with customers, internal AWS teams, and AWS partners to deliver solutions to the unique challenges of information and operational technology in telecom customers.

You will be the single-threaded owner of the AWS technology strategy for your customer and account team to drive AWS adoption. The ability to connect technology with measurable business value while assessing risks, balancing trade-offs, and encouraging risk-tasking to maximize your customer's business value of AWS is critical to a solutions architect. You should also have a demonstrated ability to think long-term about business, product, and technical outcomes.

### A day in the life

Understand customer business objectives and design solutions that are cost-effective, secure, and sustainable to deliver.

Work closely with other functions (Account Managers, Partners, Support) to delight our customers.

Build and publish external facing content.

### Basic Qualifications

- Knowledge of software development tools and methodologies
- Speak, write, and read fluently in English
- Background in any of the following: Cloud Architecture, Systems Design, Software Development, Infrastructure Architecture, Data Engineering or DevOps.
- Experience and literacy in GenAI.
- Technical Degree (Computer Science, Maths, Engineering or equivalent) and/or relevant tech experience.
- A passion for technology and for learning.

### Preferred Qualifications

- Experience working within software development or Internet-related industries
- Experience migrating or transforming legacy customer solutions to the cloud
- Experience working with AWS technologies from a dev/ops perspective
