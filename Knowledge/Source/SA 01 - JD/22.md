# DeepRec.ai — Solutions Architect

## Signal notes

The posting title splits the difference — "Mid/Senior Solutions Architect" — so I've left `seniority: unclear` rather than force a pick; the body gives no other seniority signal (no years-of-experience floor, no scope-of-book language).

This is the clearest AI-platform requisition in the batch so far: the role explicitly asks for cloud platform knowledge (AWS/Azure/GCP, unnamed which) *plus* AI/ML and MLOps literacy, plus hands-on Python/AI-framework experience as a stretch ask. That hands-on-coding bar is new relative to earlier JDs in this corpus — most presales SA roles ask for platform *knowledge*, not framework-level hands-on work. If this pattern holds across the AI-platform cluster, it's a sharper gap than the generic "no cloud cert" gap: it's coding depth, not just certification.

No named certification anywhere in the posting despite three named cloud platforms — consistent with the earlier finding that SA postings rarely gate on certs even when they gate hard on platform knowledge.

Barrier reading: this one is genuinely both platform depth *and* domain-adjacent AI/GenAI literacy — not a pure motion role. Weakest match in the corpus so far against the current moat.
