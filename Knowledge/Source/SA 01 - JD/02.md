Paired with Siemens' zero-cloud-requirement posting, that's now two-for-two against the AWS SAA spend, but for different reasons: Siemens wanted domain depth, this wants organizational-design competence.

**Not a presales requisition at all.** M&S is the employer, not a vendor selling to M&S — the role's audience is internal (Marketing, Product, Engineering, vendors), defining how the company itself adopts AI. It's the domain/governance pole opposite the presales motion in 01/Siemens, which stretches what "SA requisition" even covers in this corpus — worth flagging as a possibly-different job family rather than folding it in uncritically.

**The seniority label is the softest guess yet.** No years of experience, no title modifier ("Senior," "Principal," "Lead") appears anywhere in the posting — yet the scope (defining governance across Marketing, Product, Engineering, and vendors, company-wide) reads senior-plus. I called it `senior`; a differently-titled org could just as easily badge this `principal`.

**Domain label shares nothing with the corpus so far.** `ai-solution-design-marketing` has no overlap with Siemens' `energy-grid-software` — a second vote for keeping the domain vocabulary unfrozen through JD 10.

### About the job

We are seeking an AI Solutions Designer to define how AI capabilities are applied across content and marketing ecosystems at scale. The role translates prioritised use cases into solution patterns, platform capabilities, operating models, and governance approaches that enable teams to adopt AI safely, consistently, and effectively. Working across Marketing, Product, Engineering and vendors, the role ensures AI capabilities are integrated into enterprise platforms and ways of working in a scalable and reusable manner.

Due to high interest, this role may close earlier than advertised. We recommend applying as soon as possible.

### What You'll Do

Your key accountabilities will include:

- Define scalable solution patterns for AI-driven content creation, insight generation, and marketing capabilities.
- Design the operating models, governance frameworks, and orchestration approaches required to embed AI into content and marketing workflows.
- Assess technology ecosystems, platforms, and vendors to identify capability gaps and define the target-state architecture.
- Translate prioritised AI use cases into platform requirements, solution designs, and delivery roadmaps.
- Enable the rollout of AI capabilities across content and marketing teams by defining reusable patterns, standards, and implementation approaches.

### Who You Are

Your skills and experience will include:

- Experienced in solution design, architecture, or capability design within complex digital, content, or marketing environments.
- Able to connect business needs, technology capabilities, and operating models into clear, scalable solutions.
- Passionate about how AI can transform content creation, marketing operations, and customer experiences.
- Comfortable working across Product, Marketing, Architecture, Engineering, and external partners to drive alignment and adoption.
- A strong communicator who can simplify complexity and create practical guidance that enables teams to scale AI confidently.
