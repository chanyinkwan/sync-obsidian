# Siemens Digital Industries Software — Solution Architect (Capital)

## Signal notes

**Same parent company, opposite skill profile from JD 01.** Both are Siemens Solution Architect reqs, but where the Grid Software role (01.md) asked for zero named tooling and pure presales motion, this one demands "direct experience with Siemens Capital tools," "strong programming skills," and building "custom plugins" — hands-on product depth, not account motion. One employer, two entirely different SA archetypes; the label "Solution Architect" alone predicts nothing about the actual job.

**No cloud requirement at all — because it isn't a cloud role.** This is PLM/electrical-harness design software, deployed on-prem at customer sites for Automotive, Aerospace, and Transportation manufacturers. Irrelevant to the AWS SAA question directly, but a useful negative control: not every "Solution Architect" posting is implicitly a cloud posting.

**The domain wall is total, not partial.** "Significant professional experience in electrical harness design and manufacturing," "hands-on expertise in wire harness design, cabling systems," plus a named tool (Capital) the user has never touched — there's no adjacent surface here the way telecom is adjacent in JD 06. This is a hard miss, not a stretch, and no amount of general presales-motion rehearsal closes it.

**Seniority is a genuine coin-flip, and I called it down.** No modifier ("Senior," "Lead," "Principal") appears anywhere in the title or body — just "significant professional experience." I labelled it `mid` because the scope (own one deployment project start to finish, not a portfolio of named accounts) reads more IC than strategic, but the 60%+ international travel ask and salary band (up to ~GBP 139k) argue for something senior-adjacent. Flag for the same "true or not" review as JD 01's seniority call.

### About Siemens Digital Industries Software

Siemens Digital Industries Software is a leading provider of solutions for the design, simulation, and manufacture of products across many different industries. Formula 1 cars, skyscrapers, ships, space exploration vehicles, and many of the objects we see in our daily lives are being conceived and manufactured using our Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) software.

Are you ready to help manufacturers rethink how they design electrical harnesses from the ground up? As a **Solution Architect**, you'll work directly with customers in Transportation, Automotive, and Aerospace to explore their toughest design and manufacturing challenges, then craft solutions using **Siemens Capital** tools that create real impact on their operations. You'll be the vital link between customer needs and our technology—someone who understands their language and recognizes what success looks like for them. Based in UK with flexibility to work hybrid, you'll shape how leading manufacturers approach their most complex electrical system problems.

### Responsibilities

- Determine deployment strategies by analyzing customers' electrical system design and manufacturing needs, then document solutions that unlock the full potential of the Siemens Capital tool suite.
- Own deployment projects from start to finish at customer sites, keeping all teams in sync—consulting, sales, and engineering—to deliver on schedule and within scope.
- Lead hands-on technical workshops and training sessions that build customer team capability and confidence with Capital tools.
- Build custom plugins and configure tools to fit each customer's specific workflows and business processes, capturing their unique requirements.
- Solve technical challenges when customers encounter roadblocks, delivering solutions that strengthen their operational performance.
- Collaborate with consulting and account managers to identify new opportunities and grow the business through successful deployments.

### Qualifications

- University degree or equivalent experience in Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or a related field.
- Significant professional experience in electrical harness design and manufacturing processes.
- Hands-on expertise in wire harness design, cabling systems, and the specific challenges of Automotive, Aerospace, or Harness manufacturing.
- Direct experience with Siemens Capital tools or comparable electrical system design software.
- Strong programming skills and the ability to develop custom solutions.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills in English, both written and verbal.
- Demonstrated ability to build trust with customers and maintain strong internal networks.
- Willing to travel internationally: over 60% of time.

### Nice to have

- Knowledge of Industry 4.0 principles and digital transformation in manufacturing.
- Background in customer-facing consulting or professional services.
