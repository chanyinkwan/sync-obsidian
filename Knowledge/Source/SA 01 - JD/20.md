# SDG Group UK — AI Tech Pre-sales UK

## Signal notes

**Vendor-neutral AI/data consultancy, not a single-product vendor — hence `consulting-advisory`.** SDG Group sells cross-hyperscaler AI/data consulting services ("strong alliances with major AI hyperscalers like Microsoft, Google, and AWS") rather than owning one product. This is the clearest `consulting-advisory` fit in the batch so far, distinct from single-platform vendor SA roles (Fireblocks, ServiceNow, Ruleguard).

**By far the deepest technical bar in this batch of seven.** Named tools span Snowflake, Databricks, Azure AI Foundry, AWS SageMaker, TensorFlow, PyTorch, Hugging Face, and OpenAI tooling, plus "MLOps methodology" and "data science fundamentals." No cloud platform certification is explicitly required (only "cloud or AI architecture certifications are a plus"), so `certs: []` stands, but the depth of named ML/data-platform tooling is a strong signal that this role's actual gate is hands-on AI/ML and data-engineering fluency — a materially bigger gap than the AWS SAA alone would close. Flagged `data-engineering-depth` and `genai-hands-on-literacy` accordingly.

**Comp is unusually explicit for this corpus** — "Basic £90k + Comms + Bens" — one of the few files where a real number survived the LinkedIn paste.

**Seniority and industry both left unclear deliberately.** Title carries no modifier ("AI Tech Pre-sales UK"), and SDG's own copy describes clients "across diverse industries" with no single vertical named.

Your main focus will be on helping potential clients understand, envision, and adopt the right AI solutions to fit their unique business needs, acting as their trusted technical advisor throughout the sales process.

- AI Value Proposition: Play a key role not only in communicating SDG's AI value proposition but also in shaping and building it, influencing our go-to-market strategy
