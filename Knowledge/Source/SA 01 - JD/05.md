# Infosys — Presales Architect (Core Banking Products)

## Signal notes

**Zero cloud certification demanded, but cloud literacy is table stakes.** "Cloud technologies" appears as one line item inside a broader "technology foundation" bullet alongside Enterprise Systems and Microservices — breadth, not depth, and no AWS/Azure/GCP cert appears anywhere in the key criteria. That's another data point against the AWS SAA-as-gatekeeper theory: this presales architect role wants banking-domain fluency far more than a cloud badge.

**This is commercial presales dressed as "Solution Architect."** Strip the title and the responsibilities read like a deal-desk/bid function: RFP/RFI ownership, value-proposition definition, owning proposal sections, and periodic stakeholder reporting "through structured planners/reports." That maps closer to the user's existing commercial + pricing/deal work than to hands-on architecture.

**The domain wall is Finacle, not technology.** "Core banking products," "Finacle sales strategy," and "banking industry eco-systems" are named repeatedly — Finacle is Infosys' own core-banking platform. Without banking-vertical exposure, the technical-foundation bullet ("broad understanding of composable banking architectures") is unreachable regardless of general SA skill.

**Seniority reads principal, not senior.** Being the "primary solution and architecture advisor to key IT decision influencers," influencing "product strategy or roadmap," and acting as "a constant thread between Sales, Presales, Product and Delivery" scopes above a standard IC SA. Senior is my call, but principal is defensible — LinkedIn's own match signal on this one was low.

### Key work experience and responsibilities we are looking for

- Strengthen sales outcomes through expert pre‑sales technology consulting and close collaboration with Sales, Delivery, and Product teams
- Responsible and accountable for the Solution identification, mapping during the RFP/RFI and client pursuit.
- Define value propositions aligned to Finacle sales strategy, client business objectives, and technology requirements
- Apply an outside‑in, customer‑centric approach, bringing deep understanding of client strategy, needs, and expectations into solution design
- Act as the primary solution and architecture advisor to key IT decision influencers during pursuits, complementing Sales engagement with decision makers.
- Actively participate in pursuit activities with internal teams and prospect clients to articulate and validate the proposed solution architecture, and maintain appropriate engagement with clients and delivery teams post‑sale as required
- Handle RFP/RFI responses from solution and technology aspects to ensure accuracy, consistency, and relevance.
- Lead customer-facing technology presentations, demos, and POC engagements
- Own and drive the high level solution sections of the proposal/bid to ensure right/optimum solution is proposed
- Partner with sales on key technology aspects of pursuit documents, including client context, solution overview, and competitive positioning
- Responsible for influencing the product strategy or roadmap to meet the opportunity's timelines
- Be a constant thread between Sales, Pre sales, Product and Delivery functions until the deal closure to ensure right/optimum solution
- Keep the senior management and stakeholders updated on the pursuit specific aspects from Solution Architecture perspective
- Responsible for identifying and engaging alliance partners or identifying products or services which are continually required, but not currently offered (e.g. new product features, alternate application deployment models), based on regional needs
- Ensure proper solution documentation, business case justification, mapping of right product version/release, identification of partner solutions/Fintech solutions, readiness of required interfaces
- Build good industry connects that can be brought to Finacle in the form of prospects and partners
- Demonstrate knowledge of the market, industry trends and contribute towards Regional R&D centers.
- Demonstrate progress on pursuits, engagements, KPIs and Goals on a consistent periodic basis to the key stakeholders, through use of structured planners/reports, to ensure recognition of the efforts and progress.
- Ensure proficiency in designing and leading effective client workshops.
- Contribute to internal-external publications, Regional Analyst engagement.
- Participate in Region specific or Global industry events/forums.
- Occasional short work-related travel within and across the region
