# OutSystems — Pre-Sales & Delivery Architect

## Signal notes

**Dual-role posting: pre-sales AND billable delivery, in one head.** Unusually, this role splits 50/50 between architecture blueprinting for new deals and being the lead architect on live customer engagements (code/architecture reviews, hands-on Agentic AI implementation). Most postings in this corpus are one or the other — this is evidence that some markets fuse SA and delivery-architect scope into a single senior IC role, which changes the "SA craft" baseline if this pattern recurs.

**Certification requirement is real, but it's platform-specific, not cloud-specific.** The JD *requires* the OutSystems Architecture/Expert Developer Certification and *values* an OutSystems Agentic AI Certification — neither is in the frozen certs vocabulary, so `certs` only captures `togaf` (listed as a generic "plus," alongside an unnamed "Cloud Architect (AWS/Azure)" credential that isn't specific enough to log as `aws-sap` or `azure-solutions-architect`). Net signal: this employer gates on its own product certification, not a hyperscaler one — AWS SAA would be a nice-to-have at best here, not a qualifier.

**Highest hands-on-AI bar in the corpus so far.** Multi-agent orchestration, RAG, vector databases, prompt engineering, and "Human-in-the-Loop" workflow design are all named as practical (not conceptual) skills — this is a much deeper `genai-hands-on-literacy` gap than most postings, which only reference AI/GenAI in passing.

**Domain call is genuinely ambiguous.** OutSystems is a low-code/enterprise-app platform that has pivoted hard into "Agentic AI" positioning. I tagged `ai-ml-platform` because the JD's actual day-to-day content (agent orchestration, RAG, AI-readiness audits) is AI-centric, but `enterprise-saas` or `devtools-web-infra` (low-code dev platform) are equally defensible — flagging for retro-check if the corpus later reveals a cleaner cluster.

## Raw JD

### Position overview

The Pre-Sales & Delivery Architect plays a critical dual role in the EMEA Professional Services organization. Operating at the intersection of sales enablement and high-end project execution, this position splits focus evenly: OutSystems Professional Services Architect and Pre-Sales Support.

You will act as the technical bridge within the team. On the pre-sales side, you will drive architecture blueprinting, technical scoping, and validation to secure new enterprise business. On the delivery side, you will step directly into customer engagements to lead the technical execution of complex, Agentic AI and low-code solutions. This dual perspective ensures that pre-sales proposals are grounded in real-world delivery feasibility, and active delivery projects seamlessly execute on early pre-sales visions.

### Key responsibilities

**1. Pre-Sales Solution Architecture & Enablement**
- Technical Blueprinting & Scoping: partner with Sales and Solution Delivery Managers to translate customer business discovery into accurate, multi-phased Statements of Work (SOWs) and technical roadmaps
- Proof of Concepts (PoCs) & Validation: design and execute targeted technical demonstrations, architectural spikes, and interactive PoCs to prove solution feasibility, particularly around Agentic AI integrations and complex platform setups
- AI-Readiness Assessments: conduct deep-dive technical audits of client environments to assess integration paths, data readiness, and necessary guardrails for non-deterministic AI workflows
- Technical Pitching & Advisory: serve as the trusted technical authority during sales cycles, instilling confidence in client Enterprise Architects, CTOs, and technical decision-makers regarding architectural patterns and delivery capabilities

**2. Professional Services Delivery Architect**
- Architectural Ownership: act as the Lead Architect on billable customer engagements, owning the technical blueprint for enterprise-grade OutSystems applications and multi-agent AI ecosystems
- Delivery Excellence & Quality: perform code and architecture reviews, oversee integration quality, ensure delivery teams adhere to high standards of enterprise performance, security, and scalability
- Agentic AI Implementation: direct the practical implementation of LLM orchestration, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and "Human-in-the-Loop" (HITL) workflows on live customer projects
- Standardization & Playbooks: package learnings, custom connectors, and architectural patterns from active projects into reusable delivery playbooks to elevate delivery speed and quality across EMEA

### Requirements & qualifications

**Soft skills & behaviors**
- Dual-Mindset Agility: ability to pivot between rapid, commercially driven pre-sales discussions and deep, disciplined project delivery execution
- Consultative & Challenger Approach: comfortable challenging legacy architectural thinking, guiding clients toward scalable, AI-native patterns
- Commercial Awareness: mindful of time-to-value (TTFV), project margins, and scope boundaries in both pre-sales and delivery
- Technical Influence: skillful at building trust with internal delivery teams and executive client stakeholders

**Technical & functional skills**
- OutSystems Platform Mastery: deep, hands-on expertise in the OutSystems low-code platform, including advanced integration patterns, core architecture design, and performance tuning
- Agentic AI Architecture: practical knowledge of multi-agent orchestration, strategic prompt engineering, vector databases (RAG), and establishing token cost and data privacy guardrails
- Estimation & Delivery Methodologies: strong background in Agile/Scrum delivery, defining MVP boundaries, and creating realistic work-breakdown structures for SOWs

**Experience & education**
- 6+ years of combined experience in Enterprise Solution Architecture, Technical Pre-Sales, or Technical Delivery within an IT Professional Services environment
- Proven success in both winning complex software deals through technical leadership and successfully delivering high-value enterprise applications
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field

**Required & valued certifications**
- Required: OutSystems Architecture / Expert Developer Certification
- Valued: OutSystems Specialist Agentic AI Certification
- Plus: TOGAF, Cloud Architect (AWS/Azure), or Agile/Scrum certifications
