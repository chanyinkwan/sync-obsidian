The AWS SAA question stays open — this is exactly the kind of split the corpus review at JD 10+ needs to resolve.

**The posting title and the body disagree on seniority.** LinkedIn lists this as "Solutions Consultant," but the JD body calls the role "Solutions Consulting Lead" three times and has it "partnering with Enterprise Directors" on "complex Enterprise deals." I'm labelling `senior` on the body's authority, not the header's — a reminder that the scraped title field is not always the ground truth.

**This is compliance-gated selling, not demo-gated selling.** The core motion here is "leading technical diligence and security review processes with large organisations" — for Tier-1 bank clients, that means security questionnaires and governance walkthroughs are the sales gate, not a whiteboard or a POC. That's a genuinely different skill from the Siemens-style workshop/POC motion, and it sits closer to the deal-desk/commercial-governance muscle the user already has than to hands-on engineering.

**The employer is unnamed and the recruiter is a third party.** This was posted by "Node & Vector — Exec Search & Investor," not by the hiring company directly — the JD never names the actual employer, only "Venture-backed AI B2B SaaS whose clients include Tier-1 banks and financial institutions." That's a data point about stage/stealth, not a flaw in the note: the company field is left honest rather than guessed.

### About the job

Technical Pre-sales / Solutions Consultant at Venture-backed AI B2B SaaS whose clients include Tier-1 banks and financial institutions.

This is a great opportunity to join a well-backed, fast-growing business in cutting-edge AI B2B SAAS.

The Solutions Consulting Lead serves as the technical authority within the sales process, partnering with Account Executives to help prospects understand, trust, and confidently adopt our technology.

You will combine deep expertise in our platform, hosting infrastructure, security controls and AI capabilities with the ability to communicate clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences.

You will help clients understand how our technology works, how it is secured, and how it can address their needs, while managing complex technical diligence processes through to successful completion.

**Key Responsibilities:**

- Partner with Enterprise sales teams on strategic opportunities and complex Enterprise deals.
- Lead technical discovery conversations to understand client requirements, constraints and concerns.
- Deliver compelling demonstrations of the platform tailored to client use cases and priorities.
- Translate client requirements into realistic and practical solutions using the capabilities available within the platform today.
- Build confidence amongst both technical and business stakeholders throughout the buying process.
- Support Enterprise Directors in progressing opportunities and removing technical barriers to purchase.
- Demonstrated ability to prioritise and balance pre-sales activities, including sales demonstrations, solution workshops, and customer engagements, to maximise commercial impact and support revenue objectives.
- Strong judgement in managing competing demands, focusing effort on the highest-value opportunities while maintaining a strong customer experience.

**AI and Platform Expertise**

- Develop deep expertise in our platform architecture and product roadmap.
- Help clients understand how AI functionality operates within the platform and how it aligns with their governance and risk requirements.

**Required Experience**

- Experience in Solutions Consulting, Sales Engineering, Technical Pre-Sales, Solutions Architecture or a similar client-facing technical role.
- Proven track record supporting Enterprise SaaS sales processes.
- Experience leading technical diligence and security review processes with large organisations.
- Strong understanding of cloud infrastructure, SaaS architecture, integrations and enterprise technology environments.
- Familiarity with information security frameworks and enterprise procurement requirements.
- Experience discussing AI technologies, governance and security considerations with clients.

**Skills and Attributes**

- Curious, analytical and continuously learning about AI, security and emerging technologies.
- Strong ability to explain complex technical concepts clearly and confidently.
- Strong project management skills and attention to detail.
- Excellent written communication skills, particularly in security and technical documentation.
- Executive presence and credibility with senior stakeholders.
- Commercial awareness and understanding of how technical diligence impacts deal progression.
- Strong stakeholder management and collaboration skills.

Base salary of £90k to £100k plus bonus and package.
