# Viva.com — Strategic Partner Pre-Sales & Success Manager

## Signal notes

**No cloud platform, no certification, no named tooling stack.** Like the Siemens grid-software role, this is a full-lifecycle presales-to-success requisition (discovery through implementation, go-live, and long-term growth) that names zero cloud or technical certifications. The stated barrier is payments/fintech domain knowledge and commercial fluency with strategic merchants — not platform depth. Third data point building toward the pattern that presales-adjacent commercial roles in this corpus rarely gate on cloud credentials.

**This is explicitly full-lifecycle, not pure pre-sale.** The three responsibility blocks — implementation/project delivery, post-go-live customer success and account growth, and product feedback — mean the role owns the SOW, the go-live, *and* the account afterward. That is a wider span than a typical SA handoff-to-delivery model, closer to a hybrid SA/CSM. Worth flagging if the corpus starts distinguishing "pure SA" from "SA+CSM hybrid" requisitions.

**Work model is unambiguous and restrictive:** the posting states "this is a full time office based role" — no hybrid flexibility, unlike most of the other UK postings in this batch.

**Seniority marked `unclear`** — the title carries no modifier and the JD gives no years-of-experience minimum at all, only qualitative requirements. This is the honest answer rather than a forced guess.

**Industry marked `unclear`** — Viva.com serves "businesses across all verticals," so no single customer vertical is named as the target; `domain` is `fintech-payments` since the product itself (payment acceptance, banking services, card issuing) is unambiguous.

## Raw JD

**Location:** London, England, United Kingdom
**Work model:** On-site, full-time

### About the role

Please note this is a full time office based role.

Do you enjoy bringing together commercial thinking, solution design and customer success to win and grow strategic partnerships? If you are excited by complex payment solutions, enterprise conversations and helping major merchants unlock real value from technology, this could be your next move.

**Why should you apply?**

At Viva.com, you will have the opportunity to work on high-value strategic accounts in a fast-moving and innovative payments environment. This is a role where you can combine commercial insight, project ownership and relationship management, while working cross-functionally with teams across sales, product and technical solutions.

If you enjoy solving complex challenges, building trusted customer relationships and driving long-term success, this role offers the variety, visibility and impact to do exactly that.

### About Viva.com

Viva.com is the first full-fledged Tech Bank in Europe for businesses, operating across 29 European countries. From payments to financing, all connected, Viva.com brings together omnichannel payments, banking services, tailored financing and all-in-one business solutions on a single integrated platform.

Its offering spans payment acceptance, deposit accounts, card issuing, and a broad suite of financing solutions — including working capital and advance booking financing — alongside fiscalisation services, giving businesses across all verticals access to the tools they need to run their operations efficiently, with one provider.

Built on cloud-native technology, Viva.com pioneered and leads in Tap on Any Device technology, enabling acceptance on any Android device or iPhone, while connecting directly to all major international and local card schemes, local payment systems, and alternative payment methods across Europe. Viva Wallet.com Limited — Viva.com's UK subsidiary — is an Electronic-money institution licensed by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA FRN 989273) for the provision of E-money and related services within the United Kingdom.

### About the role

As a Strategic Partner Pre-Sales & Success Manager at Viva.com, you will play a highly visible role across the full customer lifecycle — from discovery and solution shaping through implementation, go-live and long-term growth. You will work with strategic merchants, internal sales teams and product stakeholders to deliver tailored solutions that make a real impact.

### What you will do

**Lead implementation and project delivery**

- Scope strategic account opportunities and define tailored project requirements.
- Produce Statements of Work and guide projects from onboarding through to go-live.
- Coordinate internal and external stakeholders to keep implementations on track.
- Maintain clear visibility of project milestones, dependencies and key decisions.
- Ensure testing, pilot phases and readiness checks are completed before launch.
- Help deliver a smooth and high-quality implementation experience for strategic merchants.

**Drive customer success and account growth**

- Own the post go-live success of strategic accounts and support long-term relationship growth.
- Act as a trusted advisor to merchants on integrations, reporting, reconciliation and tailored solutions.
- Identify opportunities to expand the relationship through additional products and services.
- Lead business reviews, monitor performance and provide recommendations for improvement.
- Carry out post go-live evaluations and capture lessons learned.
- Work closely with internal teams to align on shared growth opportunities across markets.

**Shape product improvement**

- Share feedback and feature suggestions based on strategic account needs.
- Support the Product team with clear business and technical input.
- Help influence future solution development based on customer and market requirements.

### Requirements

- Experience in pre-sales, customer success, implementation or project management within payments, fintech or a related technology environment.
- Strong ability to manage complex client conversations and cross-functional stakeholders.
- Commercial awareness with the confidence to support enterprise-level opportunities.
- Experience working with tailored solutions, integrations or strategic accounts.
- Strong organisational skills and the ability to manage multiple priorities effectively.
- A consultative, customer-focused approach with a proactive mindset.

### What would make you stand out

- Knowledge of payment solutions, integrations or merchant acquiring.
- A track record of helping grow customer relationships after implementation.
- Experience working closely with product and technical teams to shape solutions.
