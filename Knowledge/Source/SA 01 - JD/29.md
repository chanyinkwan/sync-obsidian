A **Bid Manager** acts as the project leader in bids, executing the process to develop an opportunity into a won contract. He/she is responsible for delivering the full proposition to the customer within the time, budget, and resource constraints and guidance given through the Rainbow reviews.

**Responsibilities**  
•    Coordinate & structure the bid response process and manage the bid team (financial architect, solution manager & architect, contract manager, risk manager, legal & compliance) from the beginning of the Sales pursuit until the handover to the Transition / Delivery team  
•    Analyse customer needs, RFP requirements and develop specific bid proposals:   
    Ensure the development of high-quality proposal documents and customer presentations  
    Participation in customer presentations and negotiations  
•    Act as project leader for the administrative process of sales pursuit:  
    Manage the bid budget  
    Ensure that all proposals adhere to the process and meet the required standards  
    Accompany the bid through key milestones of the Rainbow review (organise meetings, prepare documents, align internal stakeholders, etc.)  
    Conduct Win workshops and win-loss review sessions

**Account management**  
•    Collaborate with Sales/Business development teams such as Account directors/managers, CEPs and Business developers through the GTM process and Account Management process to help them identify and prioritise deals

   
•    Act as an advisor during the pursuit review, especially for bid qualification (risk measurement, feasibility)

**Customer service**  
•    Work in close collaboration with client stakeholders throughout the bid process (Q&As) 

**Qualifications & Experience**  
•    Experience in Bid management – 10-12 years, in a similar work environment   
•    Proven track record in converting sales opportunities into won contracts  
•    Experience in different types of deals – from 5 up to 50 ml  
•    Experience working in structured, deadline-driven environments  
•    Certifications related to Bid management are an advantage

**Business KPIs**  
Order Entry; Deal Project Margin; Cash flow from won bid; Bid budget compliance
