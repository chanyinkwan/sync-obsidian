## About the job

**Join ANS. Shape the future of cloud.  
  
**We’re looking for a **Solution Architect specialising in Cloud** to join our Pre-Sales team. This is a great opportunity for someone ready to step up from a hands-on technical position into a more **commercial, customer-facing role**.  
  
If you’ve hit the **top of your game technically** and are now looking for that next move into pre-sales; where you can influence, shape and win business, this is a brilliant opportunity.  
  
At ANS, we’re delivering some of the UK’s most ambitious cloud transformations. As **Microsoft UK Services Partner of the Year**, we sit right at the forefront of Azure, data and modern cloud platforms.  
  
**This isn’t just a technical role it’s a sales role  
  
**You’ll be part of the sales motion working closely with our sales team, mirroring their approach, building strong relationships and helping uncover opportunities.  
  
**We’re Looking For Someone Who Is  
  
**

- Commercially driven - motivated by success, outcomes and reward
- Confident identifying and shaping opportunities
- Comfortable going into real detail to build compelling solutions
- Able to balance consultative thinking with sales drive (that “blue with a red drive” mindset)  
      
    

**What You’ll Be Doing  
  
**You’ll sit at the centre of customer engagement - leading the technical story and guiding customers through their cloud journey.  
  
**Pre-sales & Solution Shaping  
  
**

- Create proposals, presentations, demos, business cases and PoCs
- Lead customer workshops, including ANS’ Navigator sessions - breaking down existing environments, understanding how they’re used, and designing what comes next
- Clearly articulate cost, value and solution design to customers  
      
    

**Customer Engagement  
  
**

- Work alongside sales teams to identify and qualify opportunities
- Run consultative discovery sessions to understand business and technical needs
- Confidently present solutions and recommendations face-to-face with clients  
      
    

**Solution Design  
  
**

- Design Azure-led cloud architectures (with AWS/Private Cloud exposure beneficial)
- Build migration strategies and transition plans
- Define target operating models and future-state architectures  
      
    

This is a shaping role, you won’t be hands-on delivering, but you’ll define **what gets built and how teams deliver it**.  
  
**Experience  
  
****What you’ll bring  
  
**

- A good technical knowledge in cloud (consultant, architect, engineer etc)
- Knowledge of cloud platform design (essential)
- Exposure to cloud migrations (planning or delivery)
- Understanding of managed services and modern cloud environments  
      
    

**Mindset & Approach  
  
**

- Ready to transition from “on the tools” into pre-sales and customer leadership
- A consultative approach - comfortable guiding workshops and asking the right questions
- Strong communication skills - able to explain complex solutions simply and confidently
- Ability to keep up with technology through continuous learning (training, podcasts, articles etc.)  
      
    

**What Makes You a Great Fit  
  
**✅ You’re driven by success, progression and reward  
  
✅ You enjoy working closely with sales and being part of a commercial team  
  
✅ You can spot opportunities and turn them into solutions  
  
✅ You’re confident leading conversations and presenting to clients  
  
✅ You’re ready to step out of delivery and into **solution shaping  
  
****Why ANS?  
  
**

- 🚀 Work on cutting-edge cloud projects across Azure & beyond
- 🤝 Be part of a high-performing, collaborative pre-sales team
- 📈 Clear opportunity to progress and grow your career into senior pre-sales roles
- 🌍 Help customers transform with the latest cloud technologies  
      
    

**A few things to consider  
  
****This Role Might Not Be Right If  
  
**

- You want to stay fully hands-on technically
- You’re not interested in the sales/commercial side of technology
- You’re not motivated by progression, impact or reward
