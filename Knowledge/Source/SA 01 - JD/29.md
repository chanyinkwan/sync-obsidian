# Atos — CGO - Presales Bid Manager

## Signal notes

This is not a Solutions Architect posting — it is a formal Bid Manager role inside Atos Group's Rainbow (their internal deal-governance/review) process. No cloud platform, no certification, no named technology is required anywhere in the posting. The barrier is process ownership at scale (deals from £5M to £50M, 10-12 years of bid-management experience) rather than technical depth, which is a different axis from most SA postings in this corpus.

The role is heavily commercial/coordination: it runs the bid team (financial architect, solution architect, contract manager, risk manager, legal/compliance) end-to-end from pursuit to handover, and owns budget, milestone governance, and win/loss reviews. It sits adjacent to the user's existing commercial/pricing deal work and cross-functional governance experience, but the 10-12 year formal bid-management bar and large-SI (consultancy) delivery environment are real gaps.

Domain labelled `consulting-advisory` since Atos is a vendor-neutral, cross-industry SI/consultancy and the product being sold is the bid/delivery capability itself, not a specific platform.

### About Atos Group

Atos Group is a global leader in digital transformation with c. 56,000 employees and annual revenue of c. €7.2 billion (at the go-forward perimeter), operating in 54 countries under two brands - Atos for services and Eviden for products and systems. European number one in cybersecurity and a leader in cloud, Atos Group is committed to a secure and decarbonized future and provides tailored AI-powered, end-to-end solutions for all industries.

The purpose of Atos Group is to help design the future of the information space. Its expertise and services support the development of knowledge, education and research in a multicultural approach and contribute to the development of scientific and technological excellence. Across the world, the Group enables its customers and employees, and members of societies at large to live, work and develop sustainably, in a safe and secure information space.

A Bid Manager acts as the project leader in bids, executing the process to develop an opportunity into a won contract. He/she is responsible for delivering the full proposition to the customer within the time, budget, and resource constraints and guidance given through the Rainbow reviews.

### Responsibilities

- Coordinate & structure the bid response process and manage the bid team (financial architect, solution manager & architect, contract manager, risk manager, legal & compliance) from the beginning of the Sales pursuit until the handover to the Transition / Delivery team
- Analyse customer needs, RFP requirements and develop specific bid proposals:
  - Ensure the development of high-quality proposal documents and customer presentations
  - Participation in customer presentations and negotiations
- Act as project leader for the administrative process of sales pursuit:
  - Manage the bid budget
  - Ensure that all proposals adhere to the process and meet the required standards
  - Accompany the bid through key milestones of the Rainbow review (organise meetings, prepare documents, align internal stakeholders, etc.)
  - Conduct Win workshops and win-loss review sessions

### Account management

- Collaborate with Sales/Business development teams such as Account directors/managers, CEPs and Business developers through the GTM process and Account Management process to help them identify and prioritise deals
- Act as an advisor during the pursuit review, especially for bid qualification (risk measurement, feasibility)

### Customer service

- Work in close collaboration with client stakeholders throughout the bid process (Q&As)

### Qualifications & Experience

- Experience in Bid management – 10-12 years, in a similar work environment
- Proven track record in converting sales opportunities into won contracts
- Experience in different types of deals – from 5 up to 50 ml
- Experience working in structured, deadline-driven environments
- Certifications related to Bid management are an advantage

### Business KPIs

Order Entry; Deal Project Margin; Cash flow from won bid; Bid budget compliance
