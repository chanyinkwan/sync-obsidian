Arize is an AI/agent observability platform (Series C, $135M+ raised), so this reads as a genuinely code-adjacent SA role, closer to "forward deployed engineer" than classic presales SA.

## Raw JD

### About Arize

AI is rapidly transforming the world. As generative AI reshapes industries, teams need powerful ways to monitor, troubleshoot, and optimize their AI systems. Arize AI is the leading AI & Agent Engineering observability and evaluation platform, empowering AI engineers to ship high-performing, reliable agents and applications. From first prototype to production scale, Arize AX unifies build, test, and run in a single workspace.

Arize is a Series C company backed by top-tier investors, with over $135M in funding and a rapidly growing customer base of 150+ leading enterprises and Fortune 500 companies, including Booking.com, Uber, Siemens, and PepsiCo.

### The Team

Arize is looking for an AI Sales Engineer (Solutions Architect) to join the team and get the product in the hands of every AI/ML organization. You'll work side-by-side with Account Executives to articulate the Arize value and overall strategy to differentiate the platform throughout the sales process and drive POCs from requirements gathering to close. The activities are diverse — one day assisting with a high-level pitch to a CDO, the next day working hands-on to develop a small proof of concept.

### What You'll Do

- Partner with account executives to articulate the overall Arize value proposition, vision, and strategy to customers
- Work closely with some of the most sophisticated GenAI teams in the world
- Act as a trusted advisor to prospective customers, while building relationships with technical and business stakeholders
- Lead product demonstrations of the Arize platform
- Advise on GenAI and ML best practices
- Lead discovery to understand prospect's GenAI tech stack to collaborate with the Sales team to construct a compelling value proposition
- Lead Proof of Concept (PoC) motions with prospective buyers
- Handle technical objections and develop strategies across sales, engineering, and product to unblock them

### What We're Looking For

- 3+ years of experience in a customer-facing role such as sales engineer, solutions engineer, or forward deployed engineer
- Strong communication skills: ability to simplify complex, technical concepts
- Proficiency in Python / Typescript
- Knowledge of LLM / Agentic frameworks such as OpenAI Agents SDK, Google ADK, LangGraph, and DSPy
- Understanding of GenAI concepts and application evaluation + development lifecycle
- A quick and self learner, undaunted by technical complexity of production AI deployments

### Bonus Points, But Not Required

- Previous experience working across and aligning Sales, Product, and Engineering
- Previous experience within a team that underwent a high growth stage
- Previous engineering experience in Data Engineering, MLOps, Kubernetes, GCP / AWS / Azure

Total compensation includes unlimited paid time off, generous parental leave plan, and others for mental and wellness support.
