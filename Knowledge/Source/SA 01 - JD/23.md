# Locus — Presales Manager

## Signal notes

**Zero cloud requirement again.** Another data point against the AWS SAA spend: this is a full presales-function-building role (an early European hire, explicitly building the playbook from scratch) and it names no cloud platform and no certification. The technical bar is domain depth (logistics, TMS, supply chain simulation) plus enough "API awareness to talk sensibly with enterprise IT" — a much lower platform bar than the DeepRec.ai role (22.md).

**This is a builder role, not a pure motion role.** Beyond the standard discovery→demo→proposal cycle, it explicitly asks the hire to build the presales function itself: playbooks, battlecards, demo assets, and structured market-intelligence feedback loops. That's a step up in scope from most JDs in this corpus — closer to a "head of presales, EMEA" role wearing a manager title.

**Strong overlap with existing moat.** RFx/proposal ownership, ROI-framework building, and multi-stakeholder account work (sales, product, technical teams) map closely onto commercial/pricing deal work and cross-functional governance already in the moat. The clear gap is vertical depth in logistics/supply chain, not SA craft.

**On-site, not hybrid** — worth flagging since most of this corpus skews hybrid; this is one of the few explicitly on-site London roles.

### About Locus

Battle-tested in 350+ deployments across 30+ countries, Locus is an agentic Transportation Management System for all-mile, all-channel, trusted by enterprises like Unilever, Nestlé, and Siam Makro.

The platform unifies orders, capacity, and carrier networks into one living plan, aligning planning, execution, and settlement so promises become proof. AI co-pilots with guardrails surface risk early and recommend the next best move to protect SLAs and reduce empty miles.

In 2025, Locus joined the Ingka Group (IKEA Retail) family, marking a major milestone in our journey. Backed by the scale and strength of IKEA, we continue to operate independently while accelerating our mission to make global supply chains faster, smarter, and more sustainable.

Since 2015, Locus has been on a mission to make logistics decision-making intelligent, sustainable, and real-world ready. Our platform has powered billions of deliveries across 30+ countries for global enterprises, driving measurable impact in cost savings, carbon reduction, and SLA performance. With the strength of the IKEA ecosystem behind us, we're scaling that impact even further.

### The Role

This is an early and important hire for Locus in Europe.

You'll take enterprise logistics problems and build a clear, compelling case for why Locus solves them — running discovery, designing solutions, and working alongside sales to get deals over the line.

Three things define the job:

- **Solution Design** — take what a client is struggling with and map it to what Locus actually does
- **Engagement Management** — own the presales process end-to-end, from first conversation to handover
- **Market Development** — know the European competitive landscape well enough to sharpen how we position and pitch

The right person has done this before in logistics tech or supply chain consulting, knows what good enterprise presales looks like, and wants to do it in a market that's genuinely moving.

### What You'll Do

**Day-to-day presales**
- Run discovery, scoping, and solution presentation across European enterprise accounts
- Keep sales, product, and technical teams pointed in the same direction through a deal
- Work with Account Executives on strategy, qualification, and close planning

**Client work**
- Get deep enough into a client's operations to design something that actually fits
- Deliver demos and executive presentations — to logistics directors and CFOs alike
- Use Locus's simulation tools to model client data and show the impact on cost, distance, SLA, and emissions in numbers

**Commercial output**
- Write proposals and RFP responses that are tight, accurate, and make the case clearly
- Build ROI frameworks that hold up with both ops teams and finance

**Market and intelligence**
- Feed what you're hearing from the market back to product and marketing in a structured way

**Building the function**
- Track the presales metrics that matter and report honestly on pipeline and conversion
- Build the toolkit, playbooks, battlecards, demo assets, that the broader team will use as we grow

### What We're Looking For

- 5+ years in presales or solutions consulting in logistics tech, supply chain SaaS, or SCM consulting
- Real experience with European enterprise accounts and the buying cycles that come with them
- Enough logistics and supply chain depth to challenge a client's thinking, not just reflect it back
- Strong written output — proposals and RFPs that are accurate and make the case clearly
- Advanced Excel and PowerPoint; comfort with scenario modelling; enough API awareness to talk sensibly with enterprise IT
