## About the job

**AI Solutions Analyst (Business Analyst) - (remote with 1 day a week in London) - £55 - 70k - excellent opportunity!**

  

**Role**

We are looking for an engaging & ambitious AI Solutions Analyst to help deliver AI-powered solutions. **This role combines business analysis, AI prompt engineering, and testing/quality assurance**. You’ll work closely with customers to understand their requirements, design effective AI prompts and workflows, and ensure delivered solutions meet customer expectations.

**Skills & Experience**

- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Excellent communication and documentation abilities
- Experience working with AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini
- Ability to assess AI-generated outputs critically and accurately
- Experience in business analysis, QA/testing and solution delivery
- Organised, detail-oriented, and customer-focused

**Desirable**

- Change management
- Experience with prompt engineering
- Familiarity with Agile delivery environments
- Understanding of AI/LLM capabilities and limitations

  

**Responsibilities**

- Gather and document customer requirements
- Translate business needs into AI prompts and workflows
- Test and validate AI outputs against requirements
- Identify issues, edge cases, and improvement opportunities
- Work with internal teams to refine and improve AI solutions
- Support successful customer delivery and adoption

  

**Excellent opportunity to join this high-velocity team crafting an AI-driven platform that’s reshaping how businesses operate. Superb team, culture and organisation!**
