## About the job

**Join ICOMAT – Revolutionising the Future of Lightweight Manufacturing!  
  
**iCOMAT is an advanced manufacturing company that invented Rapid Tow Shearing (RTS) - the only proven technology for producing defect-free curved-fiber composite parts at industrial scale. We deliver the lightest structures possible for aerospace, automotive, defense, and space, at a rate and cost efficiency that far exceeds existing composite production processes.  
  
We build and operate automated factories for composite materials, delivering components to Tier 1 suppliers and OEMs. The business has three main divisions: development of automated production machines, development of software design and production tools, and operation of the factory. We work with 25+ customers and completed the largest ever Series A in composites manufacturing to fuel a significant scale-up, with growth plans that will see the business multiply in size over the next two to three years and expand operations into the UK, Europe and the US.  
  
The business has currently more than 130,000 sqm of production capacity with manufacturing facilities in the UK (Gloucester and Swindon) and the US (Dayton, Ohio); a Hardware R&D Centre in Bristol, UK; and a Software R&D Centre in Athens, Greece.  
  
We are looking for a **Proposal Engineer** to sit at the critical interface between Business Development, Engineering, and Operations.  
  
This is a hands-on, commercially focused role where you will take customer requirements and turn them into high-quality, costed proposals that win work.  
  
You’ll own the proposal process end-to-end — from interpreting the brief through to submission — working closely with technical and manufacturing teams to ensure accuracy and credibility.  
  
**What will you be doing?  
  
**

- Proposal development

- Translate customer or BD briefs into structured, compelling proposals
- Develop technical narratives in collaboration with engineering teams
- Ensure proposals clearly communicate value, differentiation, and delivery approach

- Cost modelling & pricing

- Build detailed cost models (materials, labour, tooling, overheads)
- Work with operations to validate manufacturing assumptions
- Support pricing strategy to ensure competitiveness and margin

- Cross-functional coordination

- Act as the link between BD, engineering, and manufacturing
- Drive inputs, timelines, and internal reviews
- Ensure alignment between technical solution and commercial offer

- Customer engagement

- Support technical/commercial discussions with customers
- Be able to articulate proposals clearly and confidently
- Respond to queries and refine submissions

- Continuous improvement

- Help standardise proposal templates, costing tools, and processes
- Capture learnings from wins/losses to improve future bids

**What do we need from you?  
  
**

- Degree-qualified in engineering (materials, mechanical, aerospace, or similar)
- 1–3 years’ experience in industry (or a strong graduate with relevant exposure)
- Background or interest in composites manufacturing
- Looking to pivot toward a more commercial, customer-facing role
- Enjoys bridging the gap between technical detail and commercial strategy  
      
    

You’ll likely be someone who:  
  

- Is comfortable working across multiple teams and disciplines
- Has strong attention to detail, particularly in cost and data
- Can communicate clearly, both internally and externally
- Wants to grow into a broader commercial or business development role
