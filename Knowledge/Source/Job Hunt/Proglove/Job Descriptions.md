# Junior Solution Architect — UK+I, BeNeLux — ProGlove

## About ProGlove

ProGlove builds industrial wearable solutions that connect people and technology on the shopfloor — helping frontline workers in manufacturing, logistics, automotive and beyond work smarter, safer and more efficiently.

International company with offices in Munich, Belgrade, Chicago and Coventry. 201–500 employees (241 on LinkedIn at capture, 27% company-wide 2-year growth, 24% in Sales). Median employee tenure 4.2 years.

## Your mission

As a Junior Solutions Architect, you'll help customers transform the way they work. Working alongside experienced Solutions Architects, Sales, Product and Engineering teams, you'll support customers throughout their technical journey — from understanding their challenges to helping design and deliver solutions that create measurable value. Ideal for someone who enjoys technology, loves solving problems, and wants to grow into a trusted technical advisor.

## What you'll do

**Support customer success**
- Participate in customer workshops, discovery sessions and technical discussions
- Help understand customer processes, technical environments and business challenges
- Support product demonstrations, onboarding activities and technical training
- Assist with PoCs, pilots and customer rollouts
- Build trusted relationships through responsiveness and a customer-first mindset

**Contribute to solution design**
- Support the design of scalable customer solutions
- Prepare demo environments and technical documentation
- Help validate customer environments and implementation requirements
- Assist with technical troubleshooting alongside senior colleagues
- Document learnings and contribute to best practices

**Collaborate across teams**
- Work closely with Sales, Product Management, Customer Success and Engineering
- Translate customer needs into clear technical requirements
- Share customer feedback to help improve the product
- Contribute to internal knowledge sharing and continuous improvement

**Grow every day**
- Continuously develop technical expertise, customer consulting skills and business understanding while gradually taking ownership of increasingly complex customer engagements.

## What success looks like in the first months

- Build strong product and technical knowledge
- Become confident supporting customer meetings and technical discussions
- Deliver high-quality demos, pilots and customer support
- Contribute valuable ideas and feedback across teams
- Develop trusted relationships with colleagues and customers
- Take increasing ownership of customer opportunities while maintaining a customer-first mindset

## Your profile

ProGlove hires for potential as much as experience — curious people who enjoy learning, take ownership, and genuinely enjoy helping customers solve problems.

**You'll likely be successful if you:**
- Hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, Logistics, or a related field, or have equivalent practical experience
- Have some exposure through internships, projects, or early professional experience in pre-sales, solution engineering, consulting, software, hardware, or IT environments
- Enjoy understanding both technical and business challenges
- Communicate clearly and confidently with different audiences
- Are analytical, structured, and enjoy solving problems
- Take initiative and actively seek learning opportunities
- Work well with people from different backgrounds and functions
- Speak fluent English; additional regional languages are a plus

## Why ProGlove

**Grow with purpose** — School of Growth learning academy, dedicated learning time during working hours, mentoring/coaching from experienced colleagues.

**Work with flexibility** — flexible working hours, modern tools.

**Be part of something meaningful** — help shape technology used by global industry leaders, international collaborative environment.

**Depending on location** — competitive compensation, private health insurance and wellbeing initiatives, flexible benefits, employee referral bonuses, ProHealth wellbeing programme, country-specific benefits.
