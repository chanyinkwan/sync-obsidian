# Geotab — Solutions Engineer

## About the Company

Geotab is a worldwide leader in IoT and connected transportation solutions, holding "Great Place to Work" certification. The organization unites talented individuals dedicated to enabling business growth, improving community safety, and promoting sustainability.

The company specializes in advancing vehicle security, connecting commercial fleets to the internet, and delivering web-based analytics for fleet management. Geotab's open platform and Marketplace provide hundreds of third-party integrations for businesses seeking operational automation. Processing billions of daily data points through analytics and machine learning, Geotab enhances productivity, reduces fuel consumption, improves driver safety, and ensures regulatory compliance.

## About the Role

This position involves showcasing and customizing Geotab solutions for partners and end-users. The successful candidate will identify customer needs, develop tailored solutions, and help partners address business challenges. The role bridges sales channels, customer care, and product management to advance company strategies and objectives.

## Key Responsibilities

- Serve as Subject Matter Expert on the Geotab ecosystem, empowering partners and end-users to maximize technology utilization
- Conduct discovery, requirements gathering, technical demonstrations, and presentations
- Engage collaboratively with partners and end-users to optimize their use of Geotab solutions
- Partner with revenue-generating and product teams as key technical resource
- Ensure partner and end-user satisfaction through responsive, accurate service
- Support partner upskilling and excellence across different channels
- Analyze business challenges and identify viable Geotab solutions
- Maintain accurate documentation of customer and partner engagements
- Provide feedback to drive continuous product improvement

## Required Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Information Technology, or related field (equivalent work experience may substitute)
- Professional experience in technical fields such as software engineering, data analysis, or systems integration, preferably customer-facing
- Knowledge of telematics, IoT, APIs, and related technologies
- Strong problem-solving and critical thinking capabilities
- Cross-functional collaboration skills with sales, product, and support teams
- Excellent communication and presentation abilities for technical and non-technical audiences
- Customer-focused mindset committed to exceptional service
- Commitment to staying current with emerging technologies and market demands
- Strong organizational and time management skills for multiple priorities
- Ability to manage several projects simultaneously

## Work Environment

Geotab operates a flexible hybrid model supporting both in-person and remote work. Remote employees must maintain internet connectivity of at least 50Mbps download and 10Mbps upload speeds. Cloud-based applications and collaboration tools enable asynchronous work. The company prioritizes employee health, safety, and work-life balance through online social events and community engagement.
