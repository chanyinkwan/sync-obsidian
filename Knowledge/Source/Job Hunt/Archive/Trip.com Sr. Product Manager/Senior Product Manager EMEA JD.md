# Senior Product Manager EMEA — Trip.Biz (Corporate Travel)

## About Trip.com Group

Founded in 1999 and listed on NASDAQ in 2003 and HKEX in 2021, Trip.com Group is a leading global travel service provider comprising Trip.com, Ctrip, Skyscanner, and Qunar. Across its platforms, the Group integrates travel resources from industry partners and helps travellers worldwide pursue the perfect trip.

Four Customer Service Centres (Japan, Korea, UK, Philippines) provide 24/7 support in 19 languages; 30,000+ employees across 30 countries.

## The Role

As a Senior Product Manager, you will be responsible for the **EMEA Corporate Travel line of TRIP.BIZ**. You will lead the product by working with a large cross-functional team that includes presales, design, engineering, marketing, operations, partner relations, and finance. **Reports directly to the Head of Product.**

## In this role, you'll

- Develop and execute the business strategy for TRIP.BIZ's corporate travel segment.
- Partner closely with teams across Business Travel, Payments, and Customer Support to ensure a strong and cohesive user experience.
- Collaborate with executive leadership to define booking and revenue growth targets.
- Be accountable for achieving those targets through a well-executed product and go-to-market (GTM) strategy.
- Lead cross-functional collaboration, including presales, operations, and production teams.
- Focus on the unique needs of the European Travel Management Company (TMC) industry.
- Travel as needed to support initiatives and projects.
- Drive delivery of innovative, cutting-edge technology solutions.

## What you'll need to succeed

- **10+ years of Product Management experience**, with deep understanding and strong knowledge of local EMEA TMC products.
- Strong product thinking; experience building regional products in growth-stage environments. Proven ability to lead cross-functional teams and **build seamless collaboration with headquarters and APAC teams**.
- Lead **presales** activities: client presentations, solution demonstrations, product marketing, and **global RFP responses**. Proven implementation skills a plus.
- **Client-centric and commercially driven mindset** is essential.
- Oversee and streamline operations for efficient, effective product delivery.
- In-depth knowledge of the European Travel Management industry.
- Ability to create business plans from the ground up and execute strategies that drive growth and revenue.
- Strong communication and stakeholder management skills — external clients **and headquarters**.
- Experience with technology delivery is a plus.
- Fluent English; French or German a plus.
- Regular travel to work with global teams and partners.
- Located in London, UK.

## Why Trip.com Group

Open, inclusive international culture; rapid global growth with abundant career opportunities; internal transfer and global job-rotation programmes; learning opportunities (leadership, soft skills, professional expertise); flexible work arrangements; company-sponsored events.
