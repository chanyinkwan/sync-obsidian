# Jamf — Sales Engineer, EMEIA

**Location:** London, United Kingdom (hybrid)
**Captured:** 2026-08-17
**Source:** https://www.jamf.com/about/careers/jobs/?gh_jid=6129567004 (Greenhouse job 6129567004)

> Capture is condensed from the Greenhouse posting rather than pasted verbatim. Re-read the source before the interview.

## About Jamf

Jamf enables Apple device management for enterprises. Over 70,000 customers globally, automating deployment, management and security across Mac, iPad, iPhone and Apple TV. Recognised as a 2025 Best Company to Work For, with a stated 90% employee retention rate and a culture built on flexibility, work-life balance and autonomy.

## Role overview

Candidates must be UK-based or hold sponsorship. The Sales Engineer is "the technical voice in the room", delivering tailored product demonstrations and providing technical guidance to sales teams and customers across **Public Sector, Healthcare and Education**.

## Responsibilities

- Deliver customised product demos across Jamf Pro, Protect, Connect, School and Mobile Forensics
- Serve as the primary technical contact for accounts and partners
- Support proof-of-concept projects through to completion
- Conduct partner enablement and technical training
- Represent Jamf at industry conferences and events
- Create marketing content including webinars and social media
- Maintain CRM records and provide strategic input to regional sales teams
- Build long-term channel partner relationships

## Required qualifications

- 3 to 4 years in sales engineering or a similar technical customer-facing role
- Fluent English; an additional regional language is beneficial
- Foundation in Mobile Device Management, Apple enterprise, or cybersecurity
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience

## Preferred qualifications

- Jamf 300 or Jamf 400 certification
- Cybersecurity Fundamentals certification
- Up to 50% travel across EMEIA

## Notes

- **Why this one scores highest on the board.** Jamf sells Apple device management. Four years at Apple across three roles, plus a GBP 50,000 close won by clearing a device-management objection with MDM, is evidence no other candidate profile on this board can produce. The domain gate that screens most applicants out is the one thing that is strongest here.
- **The real cost.** Up to 50% travel across EMEIA, against the plan to move home before 2027. It is listed under preferred rather than required qualifications, so ask what it means in practice before treating it as binding.
- **Certifications.** Jamf 300 and 400 are the preferred quals and neither is held. AWS SAA-C03 is booked for August 2026 and is not relevant here. Jamf 300 is the cheapest credibility purchase available if this progresses.
- **Strategic enemies:** Microsoft Intune and Kandji in Apple enterprise management. Jamf's defence is depth on Apple specifically, which is exactly what an ex-Apple SE embodies.
- **Internal dialect to mirror once:** "the technical voice in the room".
