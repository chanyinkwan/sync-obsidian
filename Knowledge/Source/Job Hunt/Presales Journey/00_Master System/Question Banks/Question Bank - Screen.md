# Question Bank - Screen

Recruiter phone screens and asynchronous AI screenings (LinkedIn AI interview and similar) for Sales Engineer / Presales Consultant roles.

Use: `/interview-prep gate` draws from here, weighted toward the JD's keywords. Answer target is 60 seconds, 45 to 65 seconds acceptable. AI screeners score match to the ideal response, supporting evidence, and clarity, so lead with the answer and end with the link to this company.

Tags: `#anchor/*` are the five compulsory anchors, `#competency/*` are the five universal competencies, `#logistics` is screening admin that needs a rehearsed one-liner rather than a story.

## Opening and Background

- Can you describe your professional background and highlight the experiences most relevant to this role? #anchor/about-yourself
- Tell me about yourself in about sixty seconds. #anchor/about-yourself
- What does a typical week look like in your current role, and how much of it is customer facing? #anchor/about-yourself
- Is there anything else you would like the hiring manager to know about you? #anchor/about-yourself

## Motivation and Fit

- Why are you looking to move now? #anchor/why-role
- Why presales or sales engineering rather than a pure sales or pure engineering role? #anchor/why-role
- What do you know about us, and why do you want to join? #anchor/why-company
- In plain English, what do you think our product actually does and who buys it? #anchor/why-company
- Why should we hire you rather than a candidate with more direct product experience? #anchor/why-company
- Why this industry, and why now? What is changing in this market that made you look at companies like ours? #anchor/why-industry

## Behavioural - Discovery and Scoping

- Can you provide an example of a time when you successfully scoped and delivered a tailored solution for a client? How did you ensure it met their specific needs? #competency/1-discovery-scoping
- Walk me through how you run a discovery conversation. What must you leave the call with? #competency/1-discovery-scoping
- Describe a time you separated what a customer asked for from what they actually needed. #competency/1-discovery-scoping

## Behavioural - Technical Translation

- Give an example of explaining a complex technical concept to a non-technical buyer. What did you change about how you explained it? #competency/2-technical-translation
- Tell me about a demo or presentation you tailored for a mixed technical and business audience. #competency/2-technical-translation
- How have you used AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Copilot in your professional work, and how do you see agentic AI changing this space? #competency/2-technical-translation
- What is your hands-on technical level today? Name something you could build, configure, or debug yourself. #competency/2-technical-translation

## Behavioural - Objections and Buy-in

- Tell me about a time you handled a serious objection from a customer or a stakeholder. #competency/3-objection-buy-in
- Describe a situation where you had to influence a decision without formal authority. #competency/3-objection-buy-in
- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a colleague about a customer solution. How was it resolved? #competency/3-objection-buy-in
- How do you handle a customer asking for a feature the product does not have? #competency/3-objection-buy-in

## Behavioural - Commercial Judgment

- Tell me about a time your work produced a measurable commercial result. What was the number? #competency/4-commercial-judgment
- How do you build a business case for a customer? Walk me through the numbers you would use. #competency/4-commercial-judgment
- Give an example of an opportunity you decided was not worth pursuing, and how you made that call. #competency/4-commercial-judgment
- What is your experience with a qualification framework such as MEDDIC, MEDDPICC, or BANT? #competency/4-commercial-judgment

## Behavioural - Pressure and Fast Learning

- Describe a time you had to learn a new product or domain quickly to be credible in front of a customer. #competency/5-pressure-fast-learning
- Tell me about the most pressured deadline you have delivered against. What did you cut? #competency/5-pressure-fast-learning
- Tell me about a time something went wrong in front of a customer, and what you did next. #competency/5-pressure-fast-learning
- What is your biggest development area for this role, and what are you doing about it? #competency/5-pressure-fast-learning

## Logistics and Compensation

- What are your salary expectations for this role? #logistics
- What is your contractual notice period, and when could you realistically start? #logistics
- Are you authorised to work in the UK? Do you need sponsorship now or at any point in the future? #logistics
- Are you comfortable with the office or hybrid expectation and the travel this role involves? #logistics

## Questions for Them

- What questions do you have for me? #anchor/questions-for-them
