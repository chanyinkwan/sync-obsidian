# Question Bank - Hiring Manager

The round run by the person who will manage you. They already believe you can do the job on paper. They are testing judgment, delivery under interruption, and whether they want you on calls with their customers.

Difference from the screen bank: answers get interrupted, follow-ups go one layer deeper than you planned, and role-play prompts expect you to answer in character rather than describe what you would say.

Tags: `#anchor/*` are the five compulsory anchors, `#competency/*` are the five universal competencies, `#logistics` is admin that needs a rehearsed one-liner.

## Career Story and Motivation

- Take me through your career. Why did you make each move? #anchor/about-yourself
- You joined your current employer fairly recently. Why are you considering another move already? #anchor/why-role
- There is a gap in your timeline here. What were you doing, and what did you learn from it? #anchor/about-yourself
- Where do you want to be in three years, and does this role actually get you there? #anchor/why-role
- What would your current manager say your biggest weakness is? #competency/5-pressure-fast-learning

## Role Fit and Method

- Walk me through how you would run a discovery call with a prospect you know nothing about. #competency/1-discovery-scoping
- How do you structure a first demo? What do you show, and what do you deliberately leave out? #competency/2-technical-translation
- A customer asks for a 30 day proof of concept on production data. How do you scope it? #competency/1-discovery-scoping
- How do you decide a deal is not technically qualified, and how do you tell the account executive? #competency/4-commercial-judgment
- How do you split responsibilities with your account executive? Where does your work stop and theirs start? #competency/4-commercial-judgment

## Competency Probes

- Tell me about the most complex solution you have scoped end to end. #competency/1-discovery-scoping
- Give me an example of explaining an architecture decision to an executive who did not care about architecture. #competency/2-technical-translation
- Tell me about a technical win you personally caused. What would have happened without you in the room? #competency/2-technical-translation
- Tell me about a time a stakeholder actively blocked you. #competency/3-objection-buy-in
- Describe a time you changed a product or engineering decision using customer evidence. #competency/3-objection-buy-in
- Tell me about the largest deal you have influenced, and exactly what your contribution was. #competency/4-commercial-judgment
- Give an example of quantifying value for a customer who did not have their own baseline numbers. #competency/4-commercial-judgment
- Tell me about a time you were the least knowledgeable person in the room. What did you do? #competency/5-pressure-fast-learning
- Tell me about a mistake that cost time, money, or trust, and the control you introduced afterwards. #competency/5-pressure-fast-learning

## Live Scenarios and Role-play

- I am the customer, a CIO, and this is the first call. You have ten minutes. Start the discovery. #competency/1-discovery-scoping
- Explain our product to me as if I were a non-technical finance director who signs the invoice. #competency/2-technical-translation
- I am the customer and I say "this looks like every other tool we already evaluated". Respond. #competency/3-objection-buy-in
- I am the customer and I say "your competitor quoted us 40% less". Respond. #competency/3-objection-buy-in
- I am the customer and I say "the feature we need is missing, and that is a dealbreaker". Respond. #competency/3-objection-buy-in
- The account executive wants to demo next week. You think discovery is not finished. Convince me. #competency/4-commercial-judgment
- Mid-demo the environment breaks and nothing loads. I am the buyer watching you. What do you do? #competency/5-pressure-fast-learning

## Why Us, and the Job Itself

- Why us? What do you think we do better, and worse, than our competitors? #anchor/why-company
- What worries you about joining us? #anchor/why-company
- What is your read on where this market goes over the next two years? #anchor/why-industry
- What would your first 90 days look like, and how would you know they went well? #competency/5-pressure-fast-learning
- How do you think a solutions consultant on my team should be measured? #competency/4-commercial-judgment

## Closing

- What questions do you have for me about the team, the customers, or how I manage? #anchor/questions-for-them
- If we made you an offer, what would make you say no? #logistics
