# Interview Prep SOP

**Total budget: 3 hours.** Fixed. This is now a known cost, not an open-ended dread.

## The Friday-night version

Invite lands. You hit `Ctrl+Shift+I`, tracker note appears. You run `/interview-prep start`, book the slot, tell your partner, then close the laptop and sleep. Saturday you read one brief. Sunday you say your stories out loud until they land, then run the gate. 15 minutes before the call, you run the checklist. That's the whole system.

## T0 Commit

Run: `/interview-prep start <company>`
Budget: 15 min, invite night
Done means: interview slot booked ≤48h out, partner informed, tracker note exists, `research-baseline` triggered. No reading tonight.

## B1 Brief

Run: `/interview-prep brief`
Budget: 45 min
Done means: one-page HM-POV brief written and read by you. You never open the block notes.

## B2 Reps

Run: `/interview-prep reps`
Budget: 60 min
Done means: compulsory anchors + 5 competency stories said out loud at ≤60s each; selector drill run.

## B3 Gate

Run: `/interview-prep gate`
Budget: 45 min
Done means: scored mock passed (4 of 5 answers ≥3/4) and verdict logged in the tracker.

## Pre-flight

Run: `/interview-prep preflight`
Budget: 15 min before the interview
Done means: checklist all green - room claimed, partner told, notes closed, link/mic tested, water.

## Rule of thumb

Each stage announces its own budget and done-line before it starts anything. If a stage runs long, stop anyway and log where you got to - the gate is what decides readiness, not how much time you put in.
