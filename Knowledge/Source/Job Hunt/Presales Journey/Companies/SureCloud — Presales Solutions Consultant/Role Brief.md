# Presales Solutions Consultant — SureCloud

## About SureCloud

SureCloud builds a SaaS GRC (governance, risk and compliance) platform, aiming to replace legacy GRC tooling with something faster and more intelligent. The platform covers:

- Enterprise Risk Management
- Compliance Management
- Third Party Risk Management
- Data Privacy Management
- Internal Audit

Built on an event-driven architecture that scales from fast-growing companies to large enterprises.

## Why this role, right now

The differentiator is **Gracie**, SureCloud's agentic AI layer. In June 2026, IDC published a Market Note (IDC #US54613826), *"SureCloud Redefines GRC with Gracie AI: The Industry's First Cross-Domain Agentic Platform"*, citing a 70–80% productivity uplift across record management, evidence chasing and assessment analysis — framing this as GRC's shift from systems of record to systems of execution.

The presales pitch isn't "a place to store risks" — it's showing a prospect that work which used to take three weeks now takes an afternoon, and proving it live. Few competitors have made this pivot yet.

## Culture

- **Curious** — seek new knowledge, challenge assumptions.
- **Courageous** — take on complex problems, embrace change.
- **Charged** — passionate, energetic, driven for customers and each other.

## The role

Sits at the intersection of Sales, Product, Engineering and Customer Success. Understand what prospects are actually trying to fix, translate that into a solution they can see and believe, and carry that story through the sales cycle. Hands-on, trusted as a subject matter expert, with real influence on platform direction.

## What you'll be doing

- Run discovery with prospects to understand their GRC challenges, then scope and shape the fitting solution.
- Build and deliver tailored demos, workshops and PoC environments, including Gracie-led workflows that show measurable time saved.
- Respond to RFPs/RFIs and support prospect workshops with technical and functional expertise.
- Support a MEDDIC-driven sales playbook and build value-based business cases using the customer's own success metrics.
- Feed customer signal back to Product and Engineering to influence the roadmap; work with Customer Success on onboarding and long-term adoption.
- Bring energy, humour and authenticity.

## What you'll bring

- Presales, solutions consulting or sales engineering experience in a SaaS environment — solution scoping, discovery, stakeholder engagement.
- Strong working knowledge of MEDDIC; familiarity with the Three Whys is a plus.
- Genuine AI fluency — already uses Claude, ChatGPT or Copilot day-to-day for research, prep, content and drafting; curious about how agentic AI changes buyer expectations for a GRC platform. Doesn't need to be technical, needs to reach for these tools by instinct.
- Strong analytical skills — assessing requirements, mapping business processes, turning them into data-backed recommendations.
- Project management instincts to coordinate demos, PoCs and cross-functional activity without dropping threads.
- Excellent written and verbal communication, comfortable presenting complex ideas to technical and non-technical audiences.
- GRC/risk/compliance/audit experience highly beneficial; familiarity with SaaS platforms and modern cloud tech an advantage.
- Degree in a relevant field (Business, Information Systems, Computer Science) or equivalent practical experience.
- Serious about delivering results, never too serious about themselves.
