# SureCloud LinkedIn AI Screening Scope

## Outcome

Complete the real LinkedIn AI screening today after two evidence-based voice rehearsals.

This is a practical question-family scope, not a prediction of the exact wording. LinkedIn creates the interview from the job description, the hirer can edit the questions and ideal responses, and each response is rated on:

1. Match to the hirer's ideal response.
2. Supporting detail, evidence, and examples.
3. Clarity, concision, and structure.

The hiring team receives the recording, transcript, AI summary, and rating. The goal is therefore not to sound encyclopedic. The goal is to make every answer easy to transcribe, easy to score, and easy for Matthew Davies or another reviewer to trust.

## The 60-second answer architecture

### Behavioural or experience question

| Time | Job |
|---|---|
| 0-5s | Give the conclusion first. |
| 5-15s | State the situation and what was at stake. |
| 15-40s | Explain two or three actions you personally took. |
| 40-52s | Give the quantified result. |
| 52-60s | Connect the example explicitly to SureCloud. |

Use **Point - Evidence - Result - Relevance**. Aim for 110-130 spoken words. Use one example, not a career history.

### Company, product, or market question

| Time | Job |
|---|---|
| 0-8s | State the answer in plain English. |
| 8-25s | Explain the concept or customer problem. |
| 25-45s | Translate it into operational or commercial value. |
| 45-60s | Explain how a presales consultant would validate or demonstrate it. |

### Delivery rules

- Lead with the answer. Do not spend 20 seconds warming up.
- Use one or two metrics, never a list of five.
- Say **I** for your action and **we** only for the wider team.
- End with relevance to this role; do not expect the AI scorer to infer it.
- Prefer plain English over product jargon.
- If a product detail is uncertain, say, "My understanding from SureCloud's published material is..." and describe how you would validate it.
- Do not read a script in the real interview. Memorise the logic and proof points.

### Q20. What is Continuous Control Monitoring?

**60-second spine**

- Point-in-time evidence proves what was true during a review; CCM repeatedly or continuously tests whether a control is still operating.
- A connector can inspect a source system for a condition such as MFA, encryption, access review, or configuration state.
- A failed check creates evidence, an exception, ownership, and a remediation path rather than waiting for the next audit.
- Buyer value: less manual sampling, earlier detection of drift, current risk visibility, and stronger audit readiness.
- Demo it as a chain: control condition - failure - evidence - risk impact - action - executive view.

### Q21. Explain event-sourced architecture to a non-technical executive.

**60-second spine**

- Use the **photo versus video** analogy.
- A traditional current-state record is a photograph: it tells you the value now.
- Event sourcing preserves the sequence of changes, like video: what changed, when, by whom, and what followed.
- For a risk leader, that means historical context, stronger auditability, trend analysis, and a defensible explanation of how a risk or control reached its current state.
- Avoid claiming relational systems cannot retain history; the differentiator is that event history is a foundational design pattern rather than an added log.

## Voice-practice protocol

Say: **"Start the SureCloud screening. Ask one question at a time and do not show me the answer."**

For every response, the coach should:

1. Stop or alert at 60 seconds.
2. Score 1-5 for ideal-response match.
3. Score 1-5 for evidence and specificity.
4. Score 1-5 for clarity, concision, and structure.
5. Identify the single highest-value correction.
6. Ask the same question once more only when the average is below 4.

### Round 1 - core screen

Ask Q1-Q16 in random order. Do not show cues before the answer. Retest only answers below 4/5.

### Round 2 - depth and risk

Ask Q17-Q32 in random order, followed by Q33-Q40. Stop any answer that turns into a feature list or unsupported product claim.

### Round 3 - final mock

Ask eight questions with no coaching between them:

1. Q1 - introduction.
2. Q2 - why SureCloud.
3. Q6 - discovery.
4. Q8 - demonstration.
5. Q11 - MEDDIC.
6. Q13 or Q14 - AI fluency.
7. Q15 - GRC gap.
8. One random Tier B or C question.

Give the score and feedback only after all eight answers.
