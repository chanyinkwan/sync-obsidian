
walk me through how you’d structure a discovery session with a utilities client to map out one critical workflow in detail for Cogna’s AI platform?

Goal: To identify client's current pain point
First, I will align the business scope, making sure that we are aligned in which specific process we are looking into and what is the criteria of success in this process before diving into solutions.
Second, I'd map out the current system step by step by interviewing the people who are actually working in that step, asking what triggers this process, who touches this step, what is the decision they are trying to make using this system, and where exceptions happens?
Along the interview, I map their answer in three column: Data flow, decision logic and pain points. Not in the way they answer but in a quantifiable and specific way. For e,g not the "system check report" flows in but the backlog data, pass / fails, time out setting, flows in.
After that, I'd identify the smallest high-value workflow Cogna could realistically prove first,  allowing our customers to experience the value Cogna is providing.
Finally, I’d play the workflow back to the client: ‘This is how I understand the current process, these are the critical decisions, these are the systems we need to integrate with, and this is what success would look like.’ Once they validate that, we have something concrete enough to move from Discover into Define.
