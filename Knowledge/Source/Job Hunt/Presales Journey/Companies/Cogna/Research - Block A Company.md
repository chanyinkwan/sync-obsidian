## 2. What they sell, offering by offering, and what earns the revenue
Per the current cogna.co/platform page, the offering has three parts:
- **AI-Native Applications**: enterprise applications built around a customer's specific operational workflows (agent workflows for network operations, capital delivery, field coordination, asset performance management). This is the customer-facing product itself. — cogna.co/platform, fetched 21 Aug 2026
- **Lattice**: the underlying "knowledge layer" that captures an organisation's process logic, decision rules and domain expertise — the reusable infrastructure that lets Cogna build applications fast for a given customer/industry. — cogna.co/platform
- **Integrations**: connectors into existing customer systems (SAP, Salesforce, SCADA, GIS, Excel, ERP, IoT, legacy databases) so Cogna's apps sit alongside incumbent systems rather than replacing them. — cogna.co/platform
- Governance/security layer (access controls, audit trails, compliance, impact reporting) with ISO 27001, SOC2 Type II, Cyber Essentials, GDPR certifications — positioned as a trust/enablement layer, not a separately sold product. — cogna.co/platform
- **Revenue line**: the company states "**value delivered in weeks with outcome-based pricing**" — i.e. Cogna is paid on outcomes/value delivered from the bespoke AI-Native Applications it builds per customer, not a flat seat-based SaaS subscription. No further public breakdown (e.g. % services vs. % platform fee) was found — **partially unverified** on exact pricing mechanics. — cogna.co/platform, fetched 21 Aug 2026
