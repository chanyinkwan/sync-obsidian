# Interview Brief - ProGlove, Junior Solution Architect

Here is what I expect a candidate to know about us, and why you specifically fit.

## Part 1 - Five facts you must be able to say

- We are Workaround GmbH, trading as ProGlove. Munich HQ plus Belgrade, Chicago and Coventry, 200 to 250 people, around 45 million revenue, 2,000 plus enterprise customers.
- The product is a back-of-hand scanner weighing 39 to 48 grams (MARK 3, MARK Display, LEO, plus the LANA Android terminal), all run from INSIGHT, our cloud platform for config, firmware and analytics.
- The pitch is motion, not scanning: a pistol grip costs 4 to 6 seconds of wasted handling per scan. AIDC was 69.8 billion dollars in 2024, heading to 136.9 billion by 2030.
- Competitors come from four directions: NIMMSTA and HYCO on wearables, Zebra and Honeywell on ring scanners, Cognex and Keyence on overhead vision tunnels, Augmentir and Parsable on the software layer above us.
- Nordic Capital took majority ownership in 2022. The current bet is monetising IMU motion data through INSIGHT Warehouse 360, on accounts like BMW, DHL, Amazon and Lufthansa Technik.

## Part 2 - My worries about you, and your counter

**Six years of experience for a junior band. You will be bored, or gone.**
Your counter: the presales title is only months old (Huawei, Apr 2026). The six years are B2B sales, banking product and Apple operations, so this is a lateral move into a craft you are new to.

**No hardware or industrial wearables background.**
Your counter: at Apple you sold device fleets across 50 plus accounts, brought in GBP 350,000 of pipeline, and closed a GBP 50,000 broadcaster deal by upselling device management when they raised fleet-control worries. Hardware plus fleet software is our exact shape.

**Telecom and banking, not warehouses.**
Your counter: as Apple's Operation Data Analyst you walked six flagship sites, found the five hours a week of manual entry sitting at each, and removed roughly 1,500 hours a year. That is the baseline-and-ROI method our PoCs run on.

## Part 3 - Your why-company answer

It was the origin story that hooked me. Someone stood on a BMW line, watched people pick up and put down a scanner all day, and built something under 50 grams that took the motion out. That is the job I keep doing in a worse form. At Apple I found five hours a week of manual data entry sitting in six stores and removed it. ProGlove does that at scale, with hardware, software and a number the customer can see. I want to learn the architect side of it.
