## About Equinix

Equinix describes itself as "the world's digital infrastructure company," shortening the path to connectivity to enable innovation. The organisation operates a global interconnection platform supporting leading enterprises across networking, cloud and AI infrastructure.

## Job Summary

This role supports the EMEA Solutions Engineering presales organisation within the Telco and Media vertical. The successful candidate will collaborate with Sales Account Executives to understand client obstacles and develop technical solutions leveraging Equinix's data centre, network and cloud offerings. Equinix frames this as a great opportunity for someone early in their presales career, with mentorship from experienced team members on genuine customer opportunities.

## Key Responsibilities

**Customer & Sales Support**
- Collaborate with Account Executives to identify customer business needs and technical requirements
- Assist with RFI/RFP/RFQ documentation and customer deliverables
- Provide verbal and written communication during sales presentations, customer meetings and trade shows
- Coordinate presales support across Equinix products and services

**Technical Solutioning**
- Apply foundational knowledge of data centre, network and cloud products to customer scenarios
- Analyse situations and data of basic to moderate complexity, developing solutions under supervision

**Business Impact**
- Support Sales teams in acquiring new business and maintaining customer relationships
- Build relationships with internal stakeholders
- Collaborate with Pre/Post Sales Support and Operations teams

## Required Qualifications

- Foundational knowledge of data centre, network and/or cloud infrastructure concepts
- Strong written and verbal communication skills for technical and business audiences
- Ability to manage presales workflows from discovery through quoting
- Collaborative mindset with cross-functional team experience
- Bachelor's degree in a technical field (Engineering, Computer Science, Telecommunications) or equivalent practical experience
- Fluency in English required; German or additional European languages advantageous
- Interconnection experience and a telecom/media industry background are a plus
