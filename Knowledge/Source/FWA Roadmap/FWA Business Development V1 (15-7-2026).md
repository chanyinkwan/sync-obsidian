**1.4 Key Pain Points**

Identify the most significant challenges currently faced by the customer.

Commercial Challenges

* **Pricing pressure:** H3G positions their FWA service as cost\-effective solution for customers, which brings competitive pricing strategy and thin margin. The procurement team prioritize the device price over any other elements, which could introducing low product quality and hight FFR

* **Competitive pressure**: In Nordic, H3G faced the competition from emerging operators such as Nuuday. In Austria, the MVNOs emerge presents threats to H3G FWA market share as well. Without a clearer and competitive market position, H3G's cost\-effecitve brand image could be taken over easily.

Technology Challenges

* **Technology deployment delay:** H3G moves relatively slowly in advanced technology deployment. Network side, the WI\-FI 7 and SA deployment is much slower \(SA only available in Austria, trial in Italy and Sweden\), presenting the limitation to connction stability and user experiences. Furthermore, H3G has no plan to enter 6G or edge computing business until there is proven commercial mode to justify the investment.

* **Coverage limitation:** Users have strong impression of “location decides everything”,  especially in sub\-urban and rural area. Customers expect more consistant connection performance than theoretical maximum speed. In Sweden, Ireland, and some other OpCos, H3G is still working on expending the coverage and accessibility in remote areas. In Italy, WindTre faces spectrum resources limitation and network congestion.

* **Device performance:** To keep the brand image as a cost\-effective home broadband solution, the low\-cost devices caused limited performance. The compacity with current vendor \(e.g., Greenpacket &amp; eero in the UK\), the features compacity with core network \(ZTE, Ericsson\) might be challenging when introduce new vendors and models.

Operational Challenges:

* **Supply:** In current shortage of storage and chipset, it's challenging for vendors to guarantee stable supply, especially for small vendors, which could impact business continuity and bad payment terms. \(Greenpacket requries 3UK to pay full deposit before SI to lock in the supply capacity. Zyxel wasn't able to fulfill 5G FWA as promised couple of times in the past year.\)

* **Device lifecycle management:** Low\-cost products caused higher OpEx \(1\) higher customer service costs to solve the installation and usage problem \(2\) shorter lifetime led to more frequent device exchange and higher device costs \(3\) high FFR caused poor customer experience and high service costs

* **Installation complexity:** ODU \(IT\) and hybrid CPE \(AT\) installation challenges and high engineer costs.

**Scenario 1 – FWA Market Share Expansion**

Describe how Huawei can help accelerate customer growth through an improved FWA portfolio.

**Business rational &amp; Benefits:**

Huawei helps H3G accelerate FWA growth with its TCO solutions: leading innovation, excellent product quality, good performance, value\-added services, fast delivery, and low operation and mainenance cost/efforts. For example, Huawei CPE once helped 3DK acquire more market share in Denmark, becasue the good quality level up consumer satisfaction and drove more sales.

Innovative products could create revenue stream from new new scenarios. For example, Super Mifi offers portable, plug‑and‑play connectivity for users on\-the\-go and temporary sites, enabling high‑value tariff plans.

**Required Investments: **

First, investments include WiFi 7 4\*4 and WiFi 8 products planning, R&amp;D, and production line setup. Especially for Wi\-Fi 8 product needs to be the first to launch in order to gain a first\-mover advantage and secure ranging opportunies. Second, supply must be sufficient and stable, so that to meet clients demands as planned. Third, the cost of products needs to be lower to make the pricing more competitive.

**Key milestones**: include IDU to be ranged by H3G \(2026\), lock certain volume \(2026 H2 \-2027\), secure first PO, and expanding market share in all Opcos.

**Risks:**

\(1\) H3G has geopolitical concerns derived from US sanction.

\(2\) Before the launch of WiFi 8 products, our WiFi 7 products only support 2\*2, but for some Opcos \(UK\) WiFi 7 4\*4 is mandatory.

\(3\) Price competition is intense and rising memory component costs could pressure margins.

\(4\) Slow adoption of premium FWA devices among consumers.

**Scenario 2 – 5G Advanced / 5.5G Leadership **

**Business Rationale &amp; Benefits: **

High‑end 5.5G 8×8 MIMO CPE delivers better performance, especially in HK, where Huawei network is deployed. 5.5G CPE is already launched in HK last year; it has been providing better user experience than competitors do and attract hard\-core users and enables H3G to design high ARPU tariffs.

**Required Investments: **

\(1\)  Minimize the cost of 5.5G CPE.

\(2\) More persuasive marketing materials and test results showing that even not under Huawei network, Huawei 5.5G CPE still outperform other competitors.

**Risks:**

\(1\) H3G seems not have 5.5G network deployment plan in Europe so far, and there's no Huawei network in H3G European Opcos, thus our 5.5G CPE might not be unable to deliver its maximum 8\*8 MIMO performance.

\(2\) The pricing of 5.5G CPE is much higher than H3G's expected price range, and the global team might not consider products at this price range.

**Scenario 5 – AI\-Powered Smart Home **

**Business Rationale: **HUAWEI and H3G have a strategic opportunity to co\-launch a premium, value\-added AI tariff package powered by HUAWEI FWA devices. By utilizing device\-side Quality of Service \(QoS\) technology, APP Turbo feature, smart antenna selection, and AI chatbot troubleshooting , this partnership targets high\-value, heavy\-data segments—such as competitive gamers and remote workers—by offering on\-demand speed acceleration for specific applications.

**Benefits:**

For Consumers:

This provides a frictionless, 'always\-on' experience with a drastically elevated level of satisfaction. Premium users demand rock\-solid connection stability and are willing to pay a premium for it. APP Turbo delivers a superior user experience by prioritizing critical traffic: Gamers: Reduced latency and jitter via dedicated game acceleration. Remote Workers: Seamless, buffer\-free video conferencing and data transfers. And  automatic diagnosis and troubleshooting feature,  making setup and improve the FWA device much easier.

For H3G: ARPU Growth &amp; Differentiation. This initiative directly aligns with H3G’s flexible tariff and on device AI technology strategies. While H3G already offers targeted packages for youth, travelers, and competitor switchers, this package addresses an untapped, high\-value segment. Drives ARPU \(Average Revenue Per User\) growth, boosts customer satisfaction, and differentiates H3G's portfolio. And the anonymized telemetry and diagnostic data captured by the AI agent provide operators with invaluable, real\-time visibility into localized network performance, enabling smarter, more targeted infrastructure upgrades.

For HUAWEI: leverage the AI ability on device and maximum the value creation for customers. Demonstrates competitive differentiation against other hardware vendors without requiring heavy, net\-new R&amp;D investment. APP turbo feature is already built into HUAWEI’s FWA ecosystem and has proven commercial success across Orange OpCos.

**Risk:**

\(1\)  Without 5G Core integration in Europe, QoS is restricted to the device side. Acceleration limits exist without network\-side enforcement.

\(2\)  Promoted as a "reliable, high\-speed" premium tier, any instability poses a high reputation risk for H3G and strict TA demands for HUAWEI.

1. HUAWEI AI deployments in Europe faces regualtion and data security challenges

2. On\-device AI requires larger device storage, which under significant cost pressure

3. HUAWEI FWA's compacity with other smart home devices is limited, causing less potential for smart home expansion

**Required Investment &amp; Localization:**

1. More AI function required: other than QoS and automatic troubleshooting, more AI functions are required on FWA devices. Prioritize high\-value use cases such as fault detection and predictive maintenance, intelligent anomaly monitoring and early warning, performance optimization, and proactive service assurance to improve network reliability, reduce operational costs, and strengthen product competitiveness.

2. AI compliance and launch in Europe: Ensure the successful rollout of AI capabilities across Europe by addressing key enablers such as open\-source model deployment, information security, and cloud\-based services. Focus on regulatory compliance and cross\-border deployment constraints to ensure AI features can be launched and scaled successfully in core target OpCos.

3. Feature validation and benchmarking: Ensure the measurable effectiveness and customer value of AI capabilities through rigorous validation and benchmarking. Develop localized QoS application lists and comprehensive test cases tailored to target markets, and establish objective evaluation methodologies to demonstrate clear performance improvements over previous product generations without AI capabilities, while delivering visible and competitive advantages compared with leading market offerings.
