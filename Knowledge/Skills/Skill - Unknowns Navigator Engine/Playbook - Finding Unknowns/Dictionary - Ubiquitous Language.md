# Dictionary - Ubiquitous Language

## Playbook vocabulary
- **Map** — the plan, brief, or spec you're working from; the model in your head.
- **Territory** — what actually happens when you execute; it always pushes back on the Map.
- **Known Known** — a fact already established in the brief.
- **Known Unknown** — an open question with an owner, not yet answered.
- **Unknown Known** — an assumption you were making without realizing it.
- **Unknown Unknown** — a gap you only discover by doing.
- **Fast-Pass** — the urgent-mode detour: a ranked checklist, no questions asked, no stage advanced.
- **Run** — one engagement's full pass through the playbook, held in a `Run - <engagement> (<YYYY-MM>).md` note.
- **Promotion** — a Ledger tag recurring ≥3 times, proposed as a permanent edit to a technique card.
