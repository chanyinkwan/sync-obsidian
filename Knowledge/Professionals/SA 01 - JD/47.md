# Deloitte — Commercial Strategist, Technology & Transformation

## Signal notes

**No technology stack, no certification, no SA title anywhere in this posting.** This is a pure commercial/deal-shaping role sitting inside Deloitte's Technology & Transformation business — the "technology" in the title describes which business unit the deals belong to, not a technical requirement of the role itself. The entire qualifications list is finance/commercial acumen, contract-terms writing, and Excel. This is arguably the single closest-fit posting in the corpus so far to the user's existing commercial/pricing background.

**Seniority is inferred, not stated.** The title carries no modifier, but the role negotiates the firm's "highest value, most complex deals," shapes account-level framework/rate-card arrangements for the firm's most important clients, and coordinates multi-year, multi-country negotiations with Deloitte member firms abroad — that scope reads as senior individual-contributor / consultant-manager band, not entry-level. Downgrade if a JD-11+ recalibration says otherwise.

**Barrier is professional-services and consulting-context experience, not technical depth.** Deloitte explicitly says professional services experience is "desirable, but not essential" — so the real gap is less about missing a hard requirement and more about not yet having sat inside a consulting/SI commercial function specifically (`consulting-delivery-experience`), plus not yet owning C-suite-level deal relationships end-to-end (`c-suite-relationship-ownership`).

**Domain fit is clean.** Deloitte is a vendor-neutral consultancy — `consulting-advisory` is an unambiguous match, unlike several other JDs in this batch where the domain call required judgment.

## Raw JD

### Connect to your industry

Deloitte has established a clear vision to become the undisputed leader in professional services. The Commercial Strategist will help win deals and improve profitability through compelling pricing and commercial propositions for our clients. This individual will provide expert advice on all commercial aspects of the firm's client proposals, with a primary focus on higher value, more complex deals, and the firm's most important clients.

A major part of the role is supporting the business in responding to clients' requirements and challenges by providing commercial solutions that clients value. You will work closely with key stakeholders on major accounts to shape commercial arrangements both for individual engagements and when negotiating rate cards and commercial incentives at a client account level. You will also gather information regarding the competitiveness of the Deloitte offering and understand the latest trends in competitors' approaches.

The Commercial Excellence team also leads on developing firm-wide commercial initiatives — enhancing commercial knowledge throughout the firm through training initiatives and simple-to-follow commercial guidance, or working on the impact of industry-wide trends on the profitability and operating model of the firm.

This is a wide, varied, and influential role operating at the commercial heart of the Technology and Transformation business.

### Connect to your opportunity

The Commercial Strategist is part of the Commercial Excellence team supporting the Technology and Transformation business on the highest priority deals, providing a tailored bid support service. Many larger deals and framework negotiations involve working with Deloitte member firms in other countries on complex, multi-year negotiations with global clients. The team operates across all industries, building relationships, knowledge, and insights regarding the different commercial environment each industry presents, and provides advice on pricing and commercials for new products, services, or solutions developed by Deloitte.

### Core responsibilities

- Creating tailored, innovative pricing and commercial solutions for bids and proposals, demonstrating the greatest value to clients and enhancing Deloitte margin with the aim of winning more deals
- Directly negotiating and/or providing detailed support for negotiations on the more complex and challenging commercial arrangements with clients, for both account-level framework arrangements and specific bids and opportunities
- Helping account teams design and implement commercial strategies to enhance profitability and grow revenues for Deloitte's most important clients
- Leading engagement teams in how to best engage with client procurement teams and procurement processes
- Contributing to the development of commercial expertise across the firm through knowledge sharing, guidance, and best practice
- Keeping abreast of industry pricing and commercial trends
- Providing new ideas on value-adding commercial activities for Deloitte

This opportunity will likely require up to 15% travel.

### Connect to your skills and professional experience

Desirable skills and experience include:

- Degree educated, preferably in Finance, Economics, or a related business discipline
- Experience in structuring different pricing arrangements as well as the key components of a deal that can enhance profitability
- Experience working in a commercial role with "deal shaping" expertise for complex commercial opportunities in a professional services context
- Strong commercial acumen and confidence in applying this to both individual deals and framework agreements (e.g. account-level rate cards) for client accounts
- Experience with writing commercial terms for inclusion in contractual documentation
- Numerate, competent in the use of Excel
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills
- Strong team player with a collegiate style
- Desirable but not essential: professional services experience, ideally within a professional service organisation
- Flexibility to meet peaks of work inherent in the cycle of a deal
