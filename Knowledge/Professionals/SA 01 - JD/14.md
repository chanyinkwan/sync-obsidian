# Klaviyo — Presales Project Manager - AI Strategy

## Signal notes

**No cloud platform, no certification named** — but this is the first JD in the batch where "AI fluency" is itself the hard requirement rather than a nice-to-have. The posting explicitly wants "genuine, hands-on experience using AI tools" with the ability to speak to specifics of what worked and what had to be fixed — a materially higher bar than the usual "AI awareness" language, and a direct gap against the user's current profile (no hands-on GenAI tooling experience is noted in the moat/gap framework).

**This is not a classic SA role — it is presales operations/RFP-process ownership**, explicitly named "Presales Project Manager." The mandate is to build a new function from scratch around AI-driven RFP, RFI, and security-questionnaire response, sitting alongside (not as) the Presales Solution Architects and Demo Experience team. It maps cleanly onto `bid-management` / `rfx-response` / `proposal-ownership` in the canonical vocabulary, but has almost none of the technical-discovery, demo-delivery, or PoC-management content that defines a typical SA requisition — flag if the corpus wants to separate "presales ops" from "presales SA."

**Seniority marked `senior`** on judgment: it's an individual-contributor role but explicitly "the first dedicated hire in this function," expected to shape it from the ground up with "real visibility across the go-to-market organization" — high-autonomy scope despite the JD's own emphasis on IC status.

**Domain judgment call:** Klaviyo's core product is a marketing/customer-data platform sold specifically to e-commerce "creators" — `retail-commerce` (which the frozen vocabulary defines to include martech) was chosen over `enterprise-saas` since the customer base and product positioning are explicitly e-commerce-first, unlike the cross-vertical SaaS plays elsewhere in this batch.

`nice_to_haves` is empty because the posting's "Who You Are" section reads as a single undifferentiated list of requirements with no separate preferred/desirable tier — an honest empty list rather than a forced split.

## Raw JD

**Location:** London, England, United Kingdom
**Work model:** Hybrid · Full-time (may require up to 10% travel)

### About the Team and Role

At Klaviyo, our mission is to empower creators to own their growth. We're looking for a high-performing Presales Project Manager to lead a function built around AI-driven deliverables, creative problem-solving and cross-functional project management. The setting is customer questionnaires and proposals, but the day-to-day is about running creative, fast-moving delivery rather than traditional RFP processes.

This is an individual contributor role with real visibility across the go-to-market organization. You'll be the first dedicated hire in this function, which means you'll shape it from the ground up rather than inherit a fixed process. Some software and practices already exist, but the expectation is that you'll rethink them using AI, not simply operate within them. If your instinct is to question whether a step should exist at all before optimizing it, this role is built for you.

You'll find strong support and eager partners across Sales, Presales, Marketing and other parts of the business. This is a chance to build something new: a function that treats every customer response as an opportunity to produce something creative and tailored, while directly impacting revenue, win rate, and brand perception.

This role sits in the Presales organization, alongside our Presales Solution Architects and Demo Experience team. If you think in terms of projects rather than paperwork, have real hands-on AI fluency, and have a strong creative instinct for what makes a client-facing deliverable land, this role could be for you.

### How You'll Make a Difference

- Lead complex, multi-stakeholder deliverables end to end, coordinating input from Sales, Product, Legal, and Security under tight deadlines.
- Bring fresh, creative thinking to how proposals and client deliverables are structured and presented, rather than defaulting to existing templates and formats.
- Use AI to build new categories of customer-facing deliverables beyond the traditional written proposal, such as personalized microsites or interactive formats, in partnership with Sales, Marketing, and Presales.
- Bring an informed point of view on where AI can replace, accelerate, or improve existing processes, and pilot those changes.
- Partner with Sales and Go-to-Market operational leadership to ensure our process is measurable and built for maximum effectiveness and efficiency, including qualification, response rates, win rates, and turnaround times.
- Own the end-to-end RFP, RFI, and security questionnaire response process, from intake through submission, including maintaining a scalable, up-to-date knowledge base of approved answers.

### Who You Are

- You have strong project management instincts: you have experience managing complex, multi-stakeholder deliverables under deadline, ideally in a high-growth SaaS environment.
- You have genuine, hands-on experience using AI tools to speed up or improve your work, and you can speak specifically to what worked, what didn't, and what you had to fix.
- You have a point of view on how AI will change proposal and deliverable work over the next few years, and you're energized by that change rather than protective of the process you know.
- You have an eye for what makes a customer-facing deliverable feel tailored and compelling to a senior buyer, whether or not that experience comes from traditional RFP work.
- You're naturally creative: you look at an existing process or format and instinctively see how it could be reimagined, not just executed more efficiently.
- You're collaborative and low-ego — you know how to bring people together to get things done and aren't afraid to chase down answers or ask for help.
- You're excited by the idea of joining a company where your work will be visible, valued, and essential to how we win.
