# ProGlove — Junior Solution Architect UK+I, BeNeLux

## Signal notes

**Zero cloud requirement, zero certification, zero named platform.** This is another data point against the AWS SAA-as-gatekeeper hypothesis: the entry-level SA track here is graded entirely on curiosity, communication, and a technical-adjacent degree, not on any named tooling. The "you'll likely be successful if" list has no keyword bar at all.

**This is explicitly an entry ramp, not a lateral move.** ProGlove states outright it "hires for potential as much as experience" and welcomes candidates from internships or early professional exposure in pre-sales, solution engineering, consulting, software, hardware, or IT. That widens the addressable pool for a title-less career-changer more than most postings in this corpus.

**The domain is hardware-adjacent, not software-platform.** ProGlove sells wearable scanning devices to frontline manufacturing/logistics workers — closer to industrial/OT territory than a SaaS or cloud vendor. The barrier, if any, is understanding shop-floor/warehouse operating context, not a tech stack.

**Remote UK-wide.** Notably one of the few fully remote SA postings surfaced so far; most cluster around hybrid-London.

## Raw JD

**Location:** United Kingdom (Remote) · Full-time

### About the job

**Your mission**

As a Junior Solutions Architect, you'll help customers transform the way they work. Working alongside experienced Solutions Architects, Sales, Product, and Engineering teams, you'll support customers throughout their technical journey, from understanding their challenges to helping design and deliver solutions that create measurable value.

This is an ideal opportunity for someone who enjoys technology, loves solving problems, and wants to grow into a trusted technical advisor.

### What you'll do

**Support customer success**
- Participate in customer workshops, discovery sessions, and technical discussions
- Help understand customer processes, technical environments, and business challenges
- Support product demonstrations, onboarding activities, and technical training
- Assist with Proof of Concepts, pilots, and customer rollouts
- Build trusted relationships through responsiveness and a strong customer-first mindset

**Contribute to solution design**
- Support the design of scalable customer solutions
- Prepare demo environments and technical documentation
- Help validate customer environments and implementation requirements
- Assist with technical troubleshooting alongside senior colleagues
- Document learnings and contribute to best practices

**Collaborate across teams**
- Work closely with Sales, Product Management, Customer Success, and Engineering
- Translate customer needs into clear technical requirements
- Share customer feedback to help improve our products
- Contribute to internal knowledge sharing and continuous improvement

**Grow every day**

You'll continuously develop your technical expertise, customer consulting skills, and business understanding while gradually taking ownership of increasingly complex customer engagements.

### Your profile

At ProGlove, we hire for potential as much as experience. We're looking for curious people who enjoy learning, take ownership of their work, and genuinely enjoy helping customers solve problems.

**You'll likely be successful if you:**
- Hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, Logistics, or a related field, or have equivalent practical experience
- Have some exposure through internships, projects, or early professional experience in pre-sales, solution engineering, consulting, software, hardware, or IT environments
- Enjoy understanding both technical and business challenges
- Communicate clearly and confidently with different audiences
- Are analytical, structured, and enjoy solving problems
- Take initiative and actively seek learning opportunities
- Work well with people from different backgrounds and functions
- Speak fluent English; additional regional languages are a plus

Most importantly, you're eager to learn, curious about technology, and excited to grow.

### Why us

Joining ProGlove means joining a company where your growth matters just as much as your impact.

**Grow with purpose:** School of Growth learning academy, dedicated learning time during working hours, mentoring and coaching from experienced colleagues.

**Work with flexibility:** Flexible working hours, modern tools and technology.

**Be part of something meaningful:** Help shape technology used by global industry leaders, work in an international collaborative environment.

Depending on location: competitive compensation, private health insurance and wellbeing initiatives, flexible benefits, employee referral bonuses, global ProHealth wellbeing program, country-specific benefits.

### What success looks like

Success in this role isn't measured by knowing everything on day one — it's about how you learn, collaborate, and grow.

**Within your first months, you'll:**
- Build strong product and technical knowledge
- Become confident supporting customer meetings and technical discussions
- Deliver high-quality demos, pilots, and customer support
- Contribute valuable ideas and feedback across teams
- Develop trusted relationships with colleagues and customers
- Take increasing ownership of customer opportunities while maintaining a customer-first mindset

### About us

At ProGlove, we're building technology that empowers the people who keep industries moving. From manufacturing and logistics to automotive and beyond, our wearable solutions help frontline workers work smarter, safer, and more efficiently every day.

We're a company that combines innovation with execution. We move fast, take ownership, and focus on solving real problems for real customers. We foster a culture built on trust, collaboration, continuous learning, and the freedom to make an impact.
