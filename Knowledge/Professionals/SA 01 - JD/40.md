# Profound — Solutions Consultant

## Signal notes

Fast-growing AI-native startup ($1B valuation, backed by Lightspeed/Sequoia/Kleiner Perkins/Khosla) selling an "AI Search visibility" platform — the product itself is AI/GenAI-native (tracking how brands appear in ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini answers), which is why domain is `ai-ml-platform` rather than `data-analytics` even though the surface metaphor is marketing analytics. No cloud platform and no certification named; the technical bar is API/data-model fluency plus genuine curiosity about the AI-search space, not infrastructure depth.

Explicitly on-site (NYC or London), not hybrid — a notable outlier against the mostly-hybrid pattern in this corpus.

Comp is unusually explicit: $120,000–$200,000 base before commission, an early-stage/high-growth AI-startup comp anchor worth comparing against Asana's more established-SaaS £123k–£140k OTE figure.

## Raw JD

### About Profound

Profound is the marketing platform for the AI era. As people increasingly turn to ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini to decide what to buy, Profound gives brands the intelligence to see how AI represents them and the agents to act on it. Today, ~13% of the Fortune 500, plus companies like Ramp, Figma, Chime, Calendly, and DocuSign, use Profound to turn AI Search from a black box into a measurable growth channel.

Backed by Lightspeed, Sequoia, Kleiner Perkins, and Khosla Ventures at a $1B valuation, Profound is a lean, fast-moving team across NYC, SF, Buenos Aires, and London, shipping at a relentless pace and defining a new category at the biggest shift in marketing in 25 years.

As a Solutions Consultant at Profound, you will sit at the intersection of sales, product, and customer success, helping enterprise prospects and customers understand the value of Profound's platform and how it can be tailored to their needs. You will own the technical and strategic side of the pre-sales process, translating customer goals into scalable solutions that drive measurable outcomes.

### What You'll Do

- Partner with the Sales team to support discovery calls, demos, and RFPs, articulating Profound's technical capabilities and strategic value to enterprise prospects
- Lead custom solution design by mapping customer use cases to platform features, integrations, and data models to ensure a seamless fit
- Deliver high-impact product demonstrations that clearly show how Profound helps brands improve visibility across AI-driven platforms
- Collaborate with Product and Engineering to scope custom implementations, integrations, and data requirements for strategic accounts
- Develop and refine proof-of-concept engagements to demonstrate ROI and accelerate deal cycles
- Create and maintain technical documentation, solution playbooks, and demo environments to support repeatability and scale
- Serve as a trusted advisor to both customers and internal teams, connecting technical depth with marketing and AI strategy
- Gather market and customer feedback to help inform product roadmap priorities and go-to-market enablement

### Who You Are

- 3 to 5 years of experience in a solutions consulting, sales engineering, or pre-sales role within B2B SaaS, preferably in marketing technology, data analytics, or AI
- Strong technical fluency and comfort discussing APIs, integrations, data models, and workflows with both business and technical audiences
- Excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to distill complex concepts into clear, compelling narratives
- Deep curiosity about AI, search, and how brands show up in generative experiences
- Proven ability to partner cross-functionally with sales, product, and customer success teams
- Analytical mindset with skill in identifying customer needs and translating them into measurable business value
- Experience with AI tools, SEO or search platforms, or data visualization systems

### Location

This is an on-site role based in the NYC or London office, designed for builders who thrive on speed, iteration, and meaningful impact.

For this role, the expected base salary range is $120,000 to $200,000 before commission (NYC). Total compensation package includes base salary, commission, equity, and a full range of benefits and perks.
