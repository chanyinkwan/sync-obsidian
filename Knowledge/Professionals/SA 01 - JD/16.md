**Domain label is a genuine straddle.** Travtus sells an "Everyday (AI)" workflow/decision platform into the housing industry. The product itself is AI-branded, but the role is squarely business-analysis-plus-configuration work (process maps, decision trees, workflow rules), not model or ML engineering. I classified this as `enterprise-saas` (vertical business-ops SaaS) rather than `ai-ml-platform` because the day-to-day is platform configuration and requirements translation, not AI/ML craft — flagging the ambiguity per the frozen-vocabulary rule.

**No cloud platform or certification named.** Consistent with the emerging pattern across this corpus: the barrier here is SaaS configuration fluency and structured business analysis, not cloud infrastructure depth.

**Work model is internally inconsistent in the source.** The body text says "all team members spend at least three days a week in our London office" (reads as onsite-leaning), while the posting's own footer metadata says `Remote status: Hybrid`. I used the explicit footer tag (`hybrid`) but the body language is closer to onsite-with-mandatory-office-days — worth re-checking if this JD becomes a live target.

**Title had to be inferred.** The raw paste's header literally reads "**Job Title**" as an unfilled placeholder — the actual title only appears in the body ("We're looking for a Solution Consultant..."). Used that.

## Raw JD

**Location:** London (On-site, London Farringdon)
**Type:** Full-time, salary £60,000-75,000

### Job Summary

We're looking for a Solution Consultant who combines structured business analysis with strong technical fluency and deep platform ownership.

This role sits at the intersection of clients, product, and engineering. You will lead discovery, design scalable solutions using the Travtus Everyday (AI)™ platform, configure workflows and automation logic, and ensure successful deployment and adoption.

You will operate as a trusted advisor to clients — understanding their operational challenges in depth and translating them into either structured product requirements or robust platform configurations. You'll need to be comfortable discussing APIs, integrations, workflows, and system behaviour with technical teams, while maintaining credibility with senior business stakeholders.

This is not a purely functional BA role, nor a pure pre-sales role. It is a hybrid position requiring business structure, systems thinking, and hands-on solution design capability.

### About Travtus

Travtus is transforming the Housing Industry with our Everyday (AI)™ platform. We bring intelligence and automation together in one place so teams can move faster and work smarter. By delivering everyday AI decisions and workflows across the systems operators already use daily, our platform improves efficiency while enhancing the resident experience.

### About the Role

#### Client Discovery & Structured Analysis

- Lead structured discovery workshops with operational and senior stakeholders
- Understand workflows, constraints, data structures, integrations, and success metrics
- Translate ambiguous business problems into clear process maps, decision trees, and solution hypotheses
- Challenge assumptions and guide clients toward scalable, product-aligned solutions

#### Solution Design & Platform Configuration

- Design and configure workflows, rules, automation logic, and system mappings within the Travtus platform
- Convert business requirements into structured configuration and system behaviour
- Think in decision trees, edge cases, exception handling, and data dependencies
- Ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with the long-term product vision
- Own solution quality from design through deployment

#### Technical Collaboration & Integrations

- Work closely with Engineering and Product when new functionality or enhancements are required
- Translate client requirements into well-structured user stories and acceptance criteria
- Confidently discuss APIs, data schemas, integrations, and system interactions
- Support troubleshooting of configuration and data-related issues during implementation
- Validate that integrations and automation behave as intended in real-world scenarios

#### Testing, Validation & Business Outcomes

- Define acceptance criteria rooted in real business use cases
- Support UAT and validate that configured solutions reflect documented intent
- Identify gaps between expected and actual system behaviour
- Ensure measurable client value is delivered through effective solution design

#### Client Training & Enablement

- Develop and deliver structured training sessions for client teams
- Create clear documentation and enablement materials
- Ensure clients understand both how the platform works and how to optimise its impact
- Support long-term adoption through clear guidance and workflow embedding

#### Product Feedback & Continuous Improvement

- Identify recurring patterns in client needs and feed structured insights into the product roadmap
- Balance bespoke requests with platform scalability
- Contribute to internal best practices, reusable configuration frameworks, and implementation playbooks
- Help improve onboarding, deployment, and documentation processes

### Requirements

The successful applicant will be a natural problem-solver with an affinity for automation, efficiency, and AI-driven solutions with excellent communication and collaboration skills across both technical and non-technical teams. We're looking for someone analytical, technically fluent, and client-confident — capable of moving seamlessly between business strategy and system logic.

#### Must have requirements

- Proven experience in a Solution Consultant, Implementation Consultant, Technical Business Analyst, or similar hybrid SaaS role
- Strong business analysis capability — including structured discovery, process modelling, and requirement documentation
- Experience working with complex SaaS platforms
- Hands-on experience configuring systems, workflows, or rule-based automation
- Comfort discussing APIs, integrations, and data structures (no coding required, but strong technical fluency expected)
- Ability to translate business needs into structured user stories and acceptance criteria
- Experience supporting UAT and business-led validation
- Strong stakeholder management skills, including senior client exposure
- Excellent communication and presentation skills
- Confident operating in a startup or scale-up environment with evolving requirements

#### Nice to have requirements

- Experience with AI-driven, workflow-heavy, or automation platforms
- Exposure to REST APIs, webhooks, Postman, or integration tools
- Experience in housing, property, or regulated industries
- Experience developing training programmes or enablement frameworks
- Exposure to QA processes or deployment cycles

### About the Team

We are a dedicated team, working in a truly collaborative style, where everyone is heard and brings something valuable to the conversation. Constantly pushing boundaries to create groundbreaking solutions for our customers. We are fundamentally challenging the way one of the largest industries in the world operates, and our commercial success pays testament to the skill, commitment and passion that our team displays every day.

Working together in person fuels our best ideas and innovations, which is why all team members spend at least three days a week in our London office.
