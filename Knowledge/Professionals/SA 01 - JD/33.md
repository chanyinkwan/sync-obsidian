# SoftwareOne — Cloud, Data & AI Presales Consultant

## Signal notes

No cloud certification and no named cloud platform are required in the posting text itself — a LinkedIn search-tracking parameter in the scraped URL contained the string "aws required, solution associate," but that is the search query used to find the listing, not a requirement stated by SoftwareOne, so it is correctly excluded from `certs`. The actual bar is ISV licensing/solution knowledge, commercial pricing/margin work, and CRM-driven sales-cycle management — a reseller/ISV-licensing presales role more than a hands-on cloud-build one.

Domain is ambiguous: SoftwareOne resells cloud, data and AI *licenses and services* across ISVs (i.e. the product is the licensing/advisory motion, not a single hyperscaler platform). Labelled `cloud-infra` since cloud is the largest named category in the title and role, but `enterprise-saas` is a plausible alternate reading given the ISV-licensing framing — flagging the ambiguity per the domain rule.

Heavy overlap with the user's existing commercial/pricing deal-work and cross-functional governance background (margin alignment, cross-sell/upsell identification, multi-stakeholder proposal ownership). The clearest gaps are the SA title/credibility and any hands-on cloud-architecture depth, since the JD explicitly wants someone who can "advise clients on the best-fit technologies."

## Raw JD

**Location:** London, United Kingdom · Hybrid · Full-time
**Practical Information:** Reports to: Head of Cloud Services (Presales) · Contract type: Full-time · Visa Requirements: Valid Visa for UK · Language Requirements: Fluent/professional English, written and verbal

### About the job

Do you thrive on translating complex customer needs into compelling, value-driven solutions that win deals and build long-term client trust? Are you excited by shaping opportunities end-to-end, combining technical insight, commercial acumen, and strong relationships to drive meaningful business outcomes?

As a Cloud, Data & AI Presales Consultant, you will work closely with sales teams and customers to understand business needs, scope opportunities, and design tailored cloud, data, and AI solutions that deliver measurable value. Leveraging your expertise in ISV solutions, market trends, and customer environments, you will advise clients on the best-fit technologies while supporting solution positioning, opportunity qualification, and business case development.

You will act as a trusted advisor throughout the sales cycle, translating customer requirements into scalable solutions, identifying cross-sell and upsell opportunities, and supporting solution pricing and margin alignment with sales and delivery teams. Success in this role requires strong relationship-building skills and the ability to bridge business and technical discussions.

### Key responsibilities

- Collaborate with sales teams and clients to assess requirements and effectively scope and size opportunities aligned to client expectations and organizational capabilities
- Design and propose tailored license and services solutions, acting as a trusted advisor while producing clear, accurate proposals with appropriate pricing, terms, and conditions
- Support the sales team end-to-end through the sales cycle, identifying cross-sell and upsell opportunities and contributing to strategies that maximize revenue and client satisfaction
- Partner with sales to define competitive yet profitable margins, leveraging market awareness and pricing strategies to drive sustainable business growth
- Own the development of accurate and competitive quotes for complex and strategic opportunities, coordinating with internal stakeholders to ensure feasibility and alignment
- Create and maintain up-to-date solution collateral, including presentations, case studies, and white papers, to support sales execution and enhance client engagement

### Job Requirements

- Experience analyzing business challenges and translating requirements into effective technology solutions and strategies
- Strong knowledge of ISV solutions and category trends, with the ability to align solutions to customer needs
- Sales acumen and commercial awareness to identify opportunities and contribute to revenue growth
- Strong negotiation skills with the ability to achieve mutually beneficial outcomes
- Excellent verbal communication and stakeholder management skills across all organizational levels
- Experience managing sales cycles using CRM systems, sales methodologies, and analytics tools
- Strong awareness of market trends, competitive landscapes, and industry developments
- Commitment to continuous learning and staying current with emerging technologies and best practices

### Company description

SoftwareOne is a global provider of software and cloud solutions. With a presence in over 70 countries and more than 12,000 professionals, we help organizations optimize software investments, modernize applications, and unlock the value of cloud, data, and AI.

Our people are at the core of everything we do. We enable collaboration across borders, continuous learning, and opportunities to grow in a fast-evolving technology landscape.
