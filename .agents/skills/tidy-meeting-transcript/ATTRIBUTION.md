# Attribution — load only for risky transcripts

## Count before naming

A label may contain multiple people. Split before identity inference when either signal appears:

- **Deixis contradiction (hard):** a question addressed to `你` is immediately followed by a first-person answer/status inside one block.
- **Remit breadth (soft):** one label spans mutually inconsistent account, role, or chairing behaviour.

Keep the timestamp on every resulting turn. A sparse filler-only turn stays under its raw label.

## Build and disprove candidates

Enumerate relevant people from the current glossary/project instructions. For every candidate test: self-reference, presence, language/register, and whole-meeting role. The host asks for updates, assigns owners, closes topics, and controls minutes.

Topic ownership generates candidates; it does not prove why someone spoke. “Who spoke” may be evidence; “covering for X” is inference unless stated in the recording.

## Confidence gate

| Confidence | Deliverable |
|---|---|
| High | Canonical `[[wikilink]]`; hard positive or one survivor after disproof. |
| Medium | Raw label in transcript; candidate shown only in Open questions with evidence. |
| Low/coin-flip | Raw label everywhere; action owner `_TBD (Speaker N)`; no candidate name in paste-ready content. |

Never use `⚠` to launder a low-confidence name into the note. If clarification is possible, ask once in a batch; otherwise leave it unresolved.
